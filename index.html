How to use this field guide
  1. Pick an idea below. The guide shows gear that can build it.
  2. Or browse by hardware type, such as microcontrollers, AI boards, or radios.
  3. Turn on Newcomer friendly to see gear that suits first-time builders.
  4. Read the labels and meters on each card. They show openness, price, US stores, capacity, and value.
  5. Open an entry for specs in plain words, 10 to 20 build ideas, a starter checklist, and a price table.
  6. Select the star to save an entry to My List. Print it or share it.

Every entry was researched and written with help from AI. Prices are estimates and can change at any time. Check each store before you buy.
