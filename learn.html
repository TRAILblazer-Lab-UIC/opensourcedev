Field Guide Basics

Every naturalist starts somewhere. These short pages teach the basics of open-source hardware. No tech background is needed. Read them in order, or jump to the one you need.

The basics, in order

  1. What is open source? explains why some gadgets share their plans with the world.
  2. Brains: microcontrollers and small computers explains the two main kinds of boards.
  3. How a project fits together shows the five parts of almost every gadget.
  4. How code gets onto a board walks through your first upload.
  5. Plug-and-play connectors shows how to add sensors with no soldering.
  6. Radios explained covers Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, LoRa, and mesh networks.
  7. Choosing your first board helps you pick a starter.

How to read a field guide entry

Each entry in this guide is one kind of gadget. Most entries cover one chip. Many companies build boards around that chip. The entry lists those boards and the stores that sell them.
