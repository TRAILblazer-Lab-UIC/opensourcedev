BIGTREETECH SKR Mini E3 V3

BIGTREETECH (Shenzhen Big Tree Technology Co., Ltd.)

A quiet 32-bit control board that upgrades Ender 3 style 3D printers.

Field marks: Open an Ender 3 and find this postcard-sized board where the stock mainboard used to be, its four TMC2209 drivers now quieter than the printer's own fans.

Overview

The SKR Mini E3 V3 is a control board for 3D printers from BIGTREETECH. It drops into the Ender 3 and Ender 5 in place of the stock board. Four quiet motor drivers make prints much calmer to listen to. It suits printer owners who are ready to tinker with firmware and wiring.

Good fit or not?

Good for

  • Upgrading Ender 3 and Ender 5 printers
  • Adding a bed leveling probe, lights, or a touch screen
  • Running Marlin or Klipper firmware
  • Small motion projects with stepper motors

Not great for

  • First electronics projects, as it lives inside a powered printer
  • Learning to code, as setup is mostly firmware settings
  • Wireless control on its own, as it has no radio
  • Projects that need many spare pins

Specs in plain words

  • A 64 MHz chip with 512 KB of storage runs a full printer firmware with room for extras.
  • Four TMC2209 drivers move four motors smoothly with very little noise.
  • 12 V or 24 V power input matches the supplies inside most hobby printers.
  • A microSD slot updates the firmware by copying one file.
  • The board is about 10 by 7 cm, about the size of a postcard folded in half.
Full spec sheet
ProcessorSTMicroelectronics STM32G0B1RET6 Arm Cortex-M0+ (V3.0), 1 core at 64 MHz
Memory144 KB
Storage512 KB
Ways to connect partsSerial (UART), SPI, USB device
ConnectorsMicro USB, microSD slot, Pin headers, Motor ports
Built inmotor driver
PowerDC 12 V or 24 V
Size103.75 x 70.25 mm

Values are for the V3.0 board. Flash and RAM come from Marlin's STM32G0B1RE board definition (524288 and 147456 bytes). The V3.0 user manual names the STM32G0B1RET6. The V3.0 schematic PDF text names the STM32G0B1RCT6, which has less flash. This was not resolved. The V3.0.1 revision uses an STM32F401RCT6 Cortex-M4 per its schematic and Klipper notes. The board is 4 layers with 3.3 V logic. It carries a 24C32 EEPROM. gpio and ADC counts are not stated by BTT. Spare I/O is limited to the expansion headers. The motor connector entry covers the X, Y, Z (two ports) and E stepper outputs.

    Uses: Drives motors. Add: Ender 3 printer, small screwdriver set.

    Uses: Drives motors. Add: touch probe for 3D printers.

  • Glowing printer enclosure

    Add an LED strip on the light header. The lights change color to show heating, printing, and done.

    Add: LED strip.

  • Touch screen control

    Add a color touch screen to start prints and move the head by hand. It makes the printer easier for a class to share.

    Add: touch screen for 3D printers.

  • Safe shutdown with a backup supply

    Add a backup power module on the UPS header. The printer saves its place during a power cut.

    Add: backup power module.

Expert ideas

  • Faster prints with Klipper

    Connect a small Linux computer and run Klipper. It plans each move for smoother and faster prints.

    Uses: Drives motors. Add: small Linux computer, USB cable.

  • Homing without end switches

    Turn on sensorless homing with jumpers. The motors feel when they hit the edge and stop.

    Uses: Drives motors. Add: jumper caps.

  • Small pen plotter

    Drive two motors to move a pen across paper. Draw signs, cards, and art from a computer file.

    Uses: Drives motors. Add: stepper motors, rails and belts, small servo motor, 24 V power supply.

  • Camera slider for nature videos

    Move a camera slowly along a rail with one stepper motor. Film flowers opening or clouds drifting by.

    Uses: Drives motors. Add: stepper motor, rail and belt kit, 24 V power supply.

  • Mini CNC engraver

    Build a small three axis frame and drive it with the four motor outputs. Engrave wood or soft plastic.

    Uses: Drives motors. Add: stepper motors, small CNC frame, small spindle, 24 V power supply.
