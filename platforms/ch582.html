CH582

WCH (Nanjing Qinheng Microelectronics)

A cheap RISC-V Bluetooth chip with two USB ports for wireless keyboard gadgets.

Overview

The CH582 is a low-cost RISC-V chip from WCH with Bluetooth Low Energy built in. It is unusual for having two USB ports that can each act as host or device. That makes it a favorite for turning wired keyboards into Bluetooth ones. It suits tinkerers who are comfortable with C and some troubleshooting.

How the scores work

Good fit or not?

Good for

  • Turning USB keyboards into Bluetooth keyboards
  • Battery sensors that report to a phone
  • Small Bluetooth remotes and buttons
  • Learning RISC-V on a very cheap board

Not great for

  • First-time coders who want a smooth start
  • Wi-Fi projects, with only Bluetooth on board
  • Fully open Bluetooth code, with WCH's Bluetooth library shipped closed
  • One-click uploads, with a boot button dance needed each time

Specs in plain words

  • 32 KB of working memory holds a few hundred sensor readings at once.
  • 448 KB of program space fits a full Bluetooth keyboard program with room to spare.
  • Bluetooth Low Energy reaches a phone across a room on very little power.
  • Sleep current under one millionth of an amp lets a coin cell gadget wait for months.
  • Two USB ports let the board plug into a computer and host a keyboard at the same time.

Chip values are from the WCH CH583/CH582 datasheet V1.9. The 512 KB flash splits into 448 KB code, 32 KB data and 24 KB bootloader. As a result, flash_kb is the user code area. The datasheet gives a system clock range up to 80 MHz. By contrast, WeAct lists 60 MHz max. GPIO 40 and 14 ADC channels are for the QFN48 CH582M. The QFN28 CH582F on the WeAct board has 20 GPIO and 8 ADC channels. The 2-wire debug interface is WCH's own and is disabled by default. Most boards flash over the USB bootloader. low_power_ua is the lowest sleep gear. Shutdown mode goes down to 0.2 uA. Connectors and onboard parts are for the WeAct CH582F core board. Its size was not found.

How open is it?

Openness level

Open CodeOpen BoardWild Open

Level 2 of 3. Wild Open shares the most.

  • Board plansSchematics only
  • Hardware licenseOpen license
  • Coding toolsMostly open
  • The chip itselfClosed chip design

WeAct shares its core board schematic under the MIT license, but no PCB layout files were found. WCH's SDK is Apache-2.0, and open tools such as ch32fun and a Rust HAL support the chip. The Bluetooth stack ships as a closed library, and the chip core design stays closed.
