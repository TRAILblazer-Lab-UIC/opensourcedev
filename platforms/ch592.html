Overview

The CH592 is a cheap RISC-V microcontroller from WCH with Bluetooth LE 5.4. It also has USB, a real ADC, touch keys, and a driver for simple segment displays. It suits makers who know some C and want low-power Bluetooth gadgets for very little money.

Specs in plain words

  • An 80 MHz processor handles one simple job at a time. WeAct lists 60 MHz as the safe top speed.
  • 26 KB of memory is small. It fits a Bluetooth program and a short list of readings.
  • 448 KB of flash stores your program. That is plenty for most sensor gadgets.
  • Bluetooth LE 5.4 reaches phones and tablets across a room or two.
  • Sleep current starts at 0.8 microamps. A coin cell can last for months.
  • The ADC reads analog sensors such as light, soil, and pulse sensors.

GPIO and ADC counts are for the QFN28 CH592F used on the WeAct board. The QFN32 CH592X has 24 GPIO and 12 ADC channels. The datasheet lists a system clock range up to 80 MHz. WeAct's repo says 60 MHz max. As a result, the safe working value may be lower. Flash is user code flash. Total is 512 KB with data flash, bootloader and info areas. The lcd interface means a segment LCD driver. It is not a TFT controller. The CH591 is a cut-down twin with 192 KB code flash and no I2C or touch keys. Connectors and onboard parts are for the WeAct CH592F core board. Its size was not found.
