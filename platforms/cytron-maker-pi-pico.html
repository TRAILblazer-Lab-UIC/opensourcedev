Overview

The Cytron Maker Pi Pico is a carrier board for the Raspberry Pi Pico. Every pin has its own small light. Learners can see pin states without any test tools. It adds buttons, a color light, a buzzer, an audio jack, a micro SD slot, and six Grove ports. It suits students and teachers who want a cheap and friendly first board.

How the scores work

Good fit or not?

Good for

  • Learning how pins work by watching the pin lights
  • CircuitPython and MicroPython lessons on a budget
  • Grove sensor projects with plug-in cables
  • Simple sound and music projects

Not great for

  • Wireless projects out of the box. Wi-Fi needs an add-on module or a Pico W.
  • Battery or portable builds. It runs only from USB power.
  • Motor projects. The board has no motor driver.

Specs in plain words

  • The Pico chip runs at 133 MHz with two cores. It can juggle a few jobs at once, like reading several sensors and blinking lights.
  • 264 KB of working memory is room for thousands of sensor readings at once.
  • 2 MB of storage holds your programs and small data files.
  • The board is about 94 by 69 mm, close to the size of a playing card.
  • 26 pins connect to buttons, lights, and sensors.
  • The Base version takes your own Pico, Pico W, Pico 2, or Pico 2 W.

Core, clock, RAM, flash, GPIO and ADC values are for the original Raspberry Pi Pico, from the Pico datasheet. The Base version also accepts a Pico W, Pico 2 or Pico 2 W, which change these values. Speaker stands for the piezo buzzer. The ESP-01 socket on GP16 and GP17 adds Wi-Fi only when a module is fitted. As a result, radios is empty. On Rev 1.2 the buttons on GP20 to GP22 have hardware pull-ups and debounce capacitors. The buzzer and the left audio channel share GP18. Size comes from the CMU course page, which lists 93.98 x 68.58 mm with M3 holes on an 88.90 x 63.50 mm pattern.

Things you could build

13 ideas sized to what this board can do. Starter ideas suit first-time builders.

Starter ideas

  • See your pins blink

    Turn each pin on and off in a loop. The row of little lights shows a running wave.

  • Three-button piano

    Each button plays a different note on the buzzer. Write a short song and play it back.

    Uses: Makes sound.

  • Reaction timer

    The color light turns green at a random moment. Press a button fast and see your time.

  • Mood light

    Press the buttons to cycle the color light through calm or bright colors. Use it as a desk night light.

  • Pin logic lessons

    Wire a button and an LED on the headers. Students watch the pin lights to see each signal change.

    Add: jumper wires, breadboard.

Builder ideas

  • Headphone tune player

    Play simple melodies through the stereo jack. Plug in headphones or a small powered speaker.

    Uses: Makes sound. Add: headphones or small powered speaker.

  • Grove temperature display

    Plug a temperature sensor and a small screen into the Grove ports. Show the room temperature at a glance.

    Uses: Plug-in connectors. Add: temperature sensor, small OLED screen.

  • Plant thirst alarm

    Plug a soil moisture sensor into a Grove port. The buzzer beeps once the soil gets dry.

    Uses: Plug-in connectors, Makes sound. Add: soil moisture sensor.

  • SD card data logger

    Save sensor readings to a micro SD card every few minutes. Open the file later in a spreadsheet.

    Uses: Plug-in connectors. Add: micro SD card, temperature sensor.

  • Custom keyboard shortcuts

    Turn the three buttons into shortcut keys for a computer. One press can copy, paste, or mute.

  • Motion alarm

    Plug a motion sensor into a Grove port. The buzzer sounds and the color light flashes red.

    Uses: Plug-in connectors, Makes sound. Add: motion sensor.

  • Servo pointer

    Connect a servo to a Grove port. Press the buttons to swing a pointer to show the weather.

    Uses: Plug-in connectors. Add: servo motor.

Expert ideas

  • Wi-Fi sensor reporter

    Fit a small Wi-Fi module into the socket. Send readings from a Grove sensor to a web dashboard.

    Uses: Plug-in connectors. Add: small Wi-Fi add-on module, temperature sensor.
