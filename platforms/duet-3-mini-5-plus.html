Overview

The Duet 3 Mini 5+ is a controller board for machines that move. It drives five stepper motors and runs heaters, fans, and sensors for 3D printers, lasers, and small CNC machines. You set it up and control it from a web browser over WiFi or Ethernet. It suits makers building or upgrading a machine who want fully published design files.

Good fit or not?

Good for

  • Building or upgrading a 3D printer
  • Controlling a machine from a phone or laptop browser
  • Quiet and smooth motor motion
  • Growing into bigger machines with expansion boards

Not great for

  • Beginners with no machine to put it in
  • Projects that need I2C sensors, which the firmware does not support on this board
  • Small budgets, with prices around 150 to 160 US dollars
  • Shoppers in some regions, with a few stores listing it as end of life

Specs in plain words

  • A 120 MHz Arm chip with 256 KB of memory plans every move. That is ample brainpower for smooth printing.
  • Five motor drivers each push up to 1.4 amps. That covers the common stepper motors on hobby printers.
  • It runs on 11 to 25 volts from a separate power supply. A typical 24 volt printer supply fits.
  • The board measures 100 by 123 millimeters. That is a bit smaller than a postcard.
  • WiFi and Ethernet versions cost the same. Pick the one that matches your space.

The board is a machine controller. As a result, specs describe its SAME54 brain. Flash and RAM come from the SAM D5x/E5x datasheet for the P20 part. WiFi uses an ESP-WROOM-02U (ESP8266) module per the v1.02 schematic. The Ethernet version replaces it with wired Ethernet. Both are listed because the card covers both versions. Size is the Edge.Cuts outline of the v1.02 WiFi KiCad board. The footprint matches the Duet 2 WiFi and Ethernet boards. IO counts follow the product page. Seven IO ports, three thermistor inputs and four fan outputs are not single-pin GPIO. As a result, gpio and adc_channels are null. The micro USB port, the microSD socket and the MCP2542 CAN-FD transceiver come from the schematic. A PanelDue display connector and a NeoPixel output are also present. Stepper drivers deliver up to 1.4 A RMS and 2 A peak per the product page.

Starter ideas

  • Quiet printing at night

    Turn on the quiet stepper mode in the settings. Your printer can hum along softly in a bedroom or classroom.

    Uses: Drives motors. Add: 3D printer.

  • Run your printer from a browser

    Start, watch, and pause prints from a web page over WiFi. You never need to carry a memory card again.

    Uses: Wi-Fi. Add: 3D printer.

Builder ideas

  • Give an old printer a new brain

    Swap the main board in an older 3D printer for the Duet. You get smoother motion and a web control panel.

    Uses: Drives motors. Add: 3D printer, wire crimping tool.

  • School makerspace printer on the network

    Plug the Ethernet version into the school network. Teachers can queue and check prints from any classroom.

    Uses: Wired network, Drives motors. Add: 3D printer, network cable.

  • Repair cafe parts printer

    Run a shared printer that makes knobs, clips, and brackets for broken items. Volunteers watch prints from a phone.

    Uses: Drives motors, Wi-Fi. Add: 3D printer.

  • Homing without end switches

    Use the stall detection in the drivers to find the edge of each axis. It means fewer switches and wires.

    Uses: Drives motors. Add: stepper motors.

  • Art pen plotter

    Build a frame that moves a pen across paper with two motors. Send it drawings as G-code and watch it sketch.

    Uses: Drives motors. Add: stepper motors, rails and belts, pen holder.

  • Nature time-lapse camera slider

    Move a camera slowly along a rail with a stepper motor. Film flowers opening or clouds rolling past.

    Uses: Drives motors. Add: stepper motor, camera rail, belt, power supply.

  • Touch panel for printer controls

    Add a small touchscreen panel to the display connector. People can start jobs without a computer.

    Add: touchscreen control panel.

Expert ideas

  • Small laser engraver

    Build a two-axis frame that moves a laser module over wood. Engrave signs and name tags.

    Uses: Drives motors. Add: laser module, stepper motors, frame kit, safety glasses.

  • Desktop CNC carving machine

    Drive three axes on a small router to carve wood or foam. Make custom game pieces or garden signs.

    Uses: Drives motors. Add: stepper motors, small router, frame kit.

  • Sand drawing table

    Uses: Drives motors. Add: expansion boards, tool board.

    Connect a small Linux computer in SBC mode. It handles the web interface and files for the board.

Openness level

Open CodeOpen BoardWild Open

Level 2 of 3. Wild Open shares the most.

  • Board plansFull design files
  • Hardware licenseOpen license
  • Coding toolsMostly open

Duet3D publishes the full KiCad schematic and board layout under CERN-OHL-S v2. Copies must carry a Duet3D credit line and may not use its logos. RepRapFirmware is open under GPL-3.0, but the SAME54 chip is closed and the WiFi module firmware states no license.
