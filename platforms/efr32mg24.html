Overview

The EFR32MG24 is a wireless microcontroller chip from Silicon Labs. It speaks Matter, Thread, Zigbee, and Bluetooth Low Energy. It sips very little power in sleep. Makers find it on thumb-sized boards such as the Seeed XIAO MG24 and the Arduino Nano Matter.

Good fit or not?

Good for

  • Matter and Zigbee smart home sensors and switches
  • Battery gadgets that sleep most of the time
  • Bluetooth projects that talk to a phone
  • Small sound or motion models on a tiny board

Not great for

  • Wi-Fi projects, with no Wi-Fi radio on the chip
  • Cameras, big screens, or sound playback
  • Total beginners who want a no-setup first board

Specs in plain words

  • One core at 78 MHz easily handles a few sensors and a radio.
  • 256 KB of working memory is plenty for sensor readings and small programs.
  • 1.5 MB of storage holds a full Matter program with some room left.
  • A small math helper speeds up tiny AI models such as a wake word.
  • Deep sleep draws about 1.3 microamps. A small battery can last a very long time.
  • The XIAO board is about the size of a thumbnail.

Chip values come from the EFR32MG24 data sheet rev 1.1. Flash and RAM are the top options and most hobby boards use them. GPIO is the QFN48 maximum. The IADC muxes onto many pins, and the XIAO MG24 exposes 19 analog-capable pins. The chip also has a separate Cortex-M0+ radio controller, which is left out of the core count. Connectors, onboard parts and size are for the Seeed XIAO MG24, which adds 4 MB of external flash. The 1.3 µA figure is EM2 deep sleep with 16 KB RAM retention and the RTC on LFRCO. The XIAO MG24 measures 1.95 µA in its low-power state. The chip has no native USB. Boards use a separate USB bridge or debugger chip.

Things you could build

14 ideas sized to what this board can do. Starter ideas suit first-time builders.

Starter ideas

  • Bluetooth plant buddy

    Read soil moisture and send it to your phone over Bluetooth. Know which pot needs water today.

    Uses: Bluetooth. Add: soil moisture sensor.

  • Bluetooth temperature beacon

    Broadcast the room temperature every few seconds. Any phone nearby can read it with a free app.

    Uses: Bluetooth, Sips power. Add: temperature sensor, small battery.

  • Bluetooth game controller

    Wire up a few buttons and send presses to a computer. Use it for simple games or slide shows.

    Uses: Bluetooth. Add: push buttons.

Builder ideas

  • Zigbee door sensor

    Tell your smart home hub each time a door opens. It speaks Zigbee like store-bought sensors.

    Uses: Thread or Zigbee, Sips power. Add: door sensor, small battery.

  • Battery garden sensor

    Check soil and air a few times an hour and sleep in between. A small rechargeable battery can last for weeks.

    Uses: Battery ready, Sips power, Thread or Zigbee. Add: soil moisture sensor, rechargeable battery, weatherproof box.

  • Mailbox arrival alert

    Sleep inside the mailbox and wake once the door opens. Your smart home hub then sends a ping.

    Uses: Sips power, Thread or Zigbee, Battery ready. Add: door sensor, rechargeable battery.

  • Step counter badge

    Count steps with a motion sensor and share the total with a phone. Wear it on a lanyard during recess.

    Uses: Bluetooth, Battery ready. Add: motion sensor, rechargeable battery.

  • Beehive weight logger

    Weigh a beehive every hour and send the reading to a hub. A rising weight shows the bees are busy.

    Uses: Thread or Zigbee, Sips power, Battery ready. Add: load cell, load cell amplifier, rechargeable battery.

  • Smart pet feeder button

    Press a button to log each meal your pet gets. The whole family can see who fed the cat.

    Uses: Thread or Zigbee. Add: push button.

  • Wireless doorbell

    Send a Bluetooth signal from a front door button. A second board inside plays a beep.

    Uses: Bluetooth, Battery ready. Add: push button, small buzzer, rechargeable battery.

Expert ideas

  • Matter smart light switch

    Make a switch that joins a Matter smart home. Phones and voice assistants can then flip it.

    Uses: Thread or Zigbee. Add: relay module, push button.

  • Thread mesh classroom demo

    Spread a few boards around a room as a Thread network. Watch messages hop from board to board.

    Uses: Thread or Zigbee, Mesh messaging.

  • Bluetooth mesh string lights

    Link several light strings over Bluetooth Mesh. One button changes the colors on all of them.

    Uses: Bluetooth, Mesh messaging. Add: LED strip, 5 volt power supply.

  • Glass break detector

    Run a tiny sound model on the chip's math helper. It can flag the crash of a broken window.

    Uses: Sips power. Add: small microphone module.

How open is it?

Openness level

Open CodeOpen BoardWild Open

Level 2 of 3. Wild Open shares the most.

  • Board plansFull design files
  • Hardware licenseOpen license
  • Coding toolsMostly open
  • The chip itselfClosed chip design
  • OSHWACertified open hardware

The SparkFun Thing Plus Matter is certified open hardware under a CERN license. Seeed and Arduino also share design files for their boards. Code builds with free tools through the Arduino core, the Silicon Labs SDK or Zephyr. The radio stacks ship as closed Silicon Labs libraries, and the chip design stays closed.
