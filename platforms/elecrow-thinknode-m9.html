Overview

The Elecrow ThinkNode M9 is a handheld LoRa messenger with a 37-key keyboard and a 2.4 inch color screen. It comes with Meshtastic or MeshCore already installed. You can type and read messages across a mesh network with no phone and no cell signal. It suits hikers, preppers, community groups, and tinkerers who want a ready-made device.

Good fit or not?

Good for

  • Texting friends off the grid with no phone
  • Sharing GPS locations on hikes and at events
  • Emergency backup communication for families
  • Joining a local Meshtastic or MeshCore network
  • Custom ESP32-S3 handheld projects for advanced makers

Not great for

  • Long range in hilly places, with a built-in antenna and no external antenna jack
  • Swapping bands, with each unit fixed at 868 or 915 MHz
  • Adding your own sensors, with no expansion pins
  • Touch control, with buttons and keys only

Specs in plain words

  • A 2300 mAh battery gives about two to three days on standby.
  • The LR1110 LoRa radio sends short messages over long distances using very little power.
  • GPS shows where you are and lets you share your spot with your group.
  • 8 MB of extra memory and 16 MB of storage leave room for color menus, maps, and games.
  • The case is about the size of a phone at 126 by 67 millimeters and weighs 123 grams.
  • Wi-Fi and Bluetooth let it link to a phone app or a home network.

The flash chip is a ZD25Q128 (16 MB) per the wiki. The product page also lists "16GB built-in memory card", which appears to be a bundled microSD card. The wiki says this claim does not match the schematic. SPI is shared by the LR1110, LCD and microSD. I2C serves the RTC (PCF8563), magnetometer (QMC6309) and IMU (QMI8658B). A CH340K handles USB-UART. GNSS is an ATGM336H-class module with 1PPS. Weight is 123 g. The device has no user expansion header. As a result, gpio is null.

    Uses: LoRa long range radio, Mesh messaging, Battery ready.

  • Share your location on a hike

    Send your GPS position to your group as you walk. Everyone sees how far apart they are.

    Uses: LoRa long range radio, GPS, Mesh messaging.

    Find friends at a crowded outdoor event with GPS and short messages. It works even when the cell network is jammed.

    Uses: LoRa long range radio, GPS, Mesh messaging.

  • School radio science lesson

    Walk the M9 farther and farther from a base node. Map how far LoRa messages reach across campus.

    Uses: LoRa long range radio, GPS.

    Pair the M9 with the Meshtastic phone app over Bluetooth. Use your phone as a bigger screen for the same network.

    Uses: Bluetooth, LoRa long range radio.
