Overview

The patch.Init is a Eurorack module with no fixed job. You load your own program and it becomes a reverb, a sampler, an oscillator, or anything else you write. Inside sits the Daisy Patch Submodule with a fast audio chip and lots of memory. It suits synth players and sound designers who want to build their own effects.

Specs in plain words

  • A 480 MHz processor handles heavy sound effects in real time.
  • 64 MB of memory can hold several minutes of recorded sound.
  • 96 kHz, 24-bit audio means studio-quality sound in and out.
  • Four CV inputs and four knobs let other modules and your hands shape the sound.
  • Two gate inputs and two gate outputs send and receive on and off rhythm pulses.
  • A microSD slot stores samples and settings.

Specs are for the Patch Submodule that sits on the back of the module. psram_kb holds the 64 MB external SDRAM. flash_kb is the 8 MB IS25LP064A QSPI flash named in libDaisy's patch_sm driver. The MCU part and the PCM3060 codec come from libDaisy source. They are not named on the product page. gpio and adc_channels are the submodule totals. The patch.Init front panel exposes 4 CV ins, 4 knobs, 1 CV out, 2 gate ins and 2 gate outs. The +12 V draw depends on the firmware. Width is 10HP and depth is 31 mm.

    Uses: Hears sound, Makes sound. Add: patch cables.

    Store drum hits on a microSD card. Each gate pulse fires a kick or snare from the speakers.

    Uses: Makes sound. Add: microSD card, patch cables.

    Send slowly changing voltages out to other modules. They wander and keep a patch sounding alive.

    Uses: Hears sound, Makes sound. Add: patch cables.

    Add: patch cables.

  • Nature sound station

    Uses: Makes sound. Add: microSD card, patch cables.
