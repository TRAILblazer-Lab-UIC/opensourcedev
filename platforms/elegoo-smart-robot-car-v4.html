Overview

The ELEGOO Smart Robot Car V4 is a robot kit for learning Arduino. Its brain is a clone of the Arduino UNO. A camera module streams video to a phone over Wi-Fi. It suits teens and adults who want to learn real Arduino code with a fun robot.

Good fit or not?

Good for

  • Learning Arduino code with a moving robot
  • Classic robot lessons such as line following and obstacle dodging
  • Driving around with a live camera view on a phone
  • Building with plug-in parts and no soldering

Not great for

  • Big or complex programs, as the UNO brain has very little memory
  • Rough outdoor ground, as it is a light indoor car
  • Very young kids, as the kit is rated for ages 13 and up
  • Quick setup, as building and matching the right program takes time

Specs in plain words

  • 16 MHz is a slow but steady speed. It runs one simple robot behavior at a time.
  • 2 KB of working memory is tiny. Short sketches fit, but big programs do not.
  • 32 KB of storage holds the main robot sketch with a little room to spare.
  • The ESP32 camera board has its own Wi-Fi. Your phone joins it directly with no home router.
  • The gyro senses turning. It helps the car drive in a straight line.
  • Plug-in ports connect each module with a cable. No soldering is needed.

Core specs are for the ATmega328P on the UNO R3 (32 KB flash, 2 KB SRAM, 16 MHz, 20 digital and analog IO, 6 analog inputs). The camera module is an ESP32-WROVER board with a CH340C USB serial chip. Its Wi-Fi is the only radio. ReadMeFirst says chip shortages led ELEGOO to swap the DRV8835 driver for TB6612, the MPU6050 gyro for QMI8658C, and the HT42B534 USB serial chip for CH340. As a result, pick the matching program. Battery chemistry, voltage and capacity were not confirmed on a fetched page. Size and weight of the built car were not found.

Starter ideas

  • Drive it with the phone app

    Switch the camera to cam mode and join its Wi-Fi network. Steer the car from your phone and watch the live video.

    Uses: Wi-Fi, Camera, Drives motors, Battery ready.

  • Tape track line follower

    Lay dark tape on the floor in a loop. The line sensor keeps the car on the track.

    Uses: Drives motors, Sensors built in, Battery ready. Add: dark electrical tape.

  • Obstacle dodging explorer

    The ultrasonic sensor looks left and right on its servo. The car turns away from walls and furniture.

    Uses: Drives motors, Sensors built in, Battery ready.

  • Remote control race

    Use the IR remote to drive through a cardboard course. Friends take turns and time each lap.

    Uses: Drives motors, Battery ready. Add: cardboard boxes.

  • Follow me pet robot

    The car keeps a set distance from your hand or feet. It trails behind you like a puppy.

    Uses: Drives motors, Sensors built in, Battery ready.

  • Mood light color changer

    Change the color of the RGB light in the sketch. Make the car glow red as it backs up.

    Uses: Sensors built in.

    Uses: Wi-Fi, Camera, Drives motors, Battery ready.

  • Garden path patrol

    Send the car along a smooth patio and watch the plants on the phone. Check on seedlings from a chair.

    Uses: Wi-Fi, Camera, Drives motors, Battery ready.

  • Maze solving robot

    Uses: Drives motors, Sensors built in, Battery ready. Add: cardboard.

  • Robot delivery to a classmate
