Overview

The ESP32-C61 is a low-cost Espressif chip with 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi 6 and Bluetooth LE 5. It runs one RISC-V core at up to 160 MHz. Some versions tuck extra PSRAM memory inside the chip, which is rare for its price. It suits makers who want cheap Wi-Fi 6 sensors and smart home gadgets.

Good fit or not?

Good for

  • Cheap Wi-Fi 6 sensors that share a busy home network
  • Battery gadgets that wake up only to report
  • Long-range Bluetooth links across a yard
  • Simple sound projects such as chimes and noise meters
  • Adding Wi-Fi 6 to a bigger processor

Not great for

  • Thread or Zigbee smart home networks
  • MicroPython users, with no build available yet
  • Addressable LED strips without extra coding tricks
  • Beginners who want lots of ready tutorials

Specs in plain words

  • Wi-Fi 6 helps many gadgets share one router and lets sensors sleep longer between check-ins.
  • 320 KB of memory plus 2 MB of PSRAM holds a small web page and plenty of sensor data.
  • 8 MB of flash on the dev board stores your program and files.
  • 30 GPIO pins give room for buttons, sensors, and lights.
  • 10 µA deep sleep lets a battery sensor run for months.
  • Bluetooth Coded PHY trades speed for extra range.

Chip values come from the ESP32-C61 datasheet v1.1 (2026-06-23). It lists 320 KB SRAM, 256 KB ROM, 30 GPIO and a 4-channel 12-bit ADC. Flash and PSRAM are for the DevKitC-1-N8R2, which uses the ESP32-C61HR2 chip with 2 MB PSRAM and 8 MB flash in the WROOM-1 module. Chip variants are HF4 (4 MB flash), HR2 (2 MB PSRAM), HR8 (8 MB PSRAM) and NF8R8LA (8 MB flash and 8 MB PSRAM. It is not yet in mass production). The chip has no RMT and no CAN controller. USB is full speed only. Connectors and onboard parts are for the DevKitC-1, which has two USB-C ports. Board size was not stated on the pages read.

Things you could build

12 ideas sized to what this board can do. Starter ideas suit first-time builders.

Starter ideas

  • Wi-Fi 6 weather station

    Read temperature, humidity, and air pressure and post them to a small web page. Check the porch from your phone.

    Uses: Wi-Fi. Add: temperature humidity and pressure sensor.

  • Bluetooth room beacon

    Broadcast the room temperature over Bluetooth every few seconds. Any phone nearby can read it.

    Uses: Bluetooth. Add: temperature sensor.

  • Smart lamp switch

    Switch a lamp on and off from a web page with a relay. It works like a homemade smart plug.

    Uses: Wi-Fi. Add: relay module, lamp.

Builder ideas

  • Long-sleep door sensor

    Report each time a door opens over Wi-Fi 6. The chip naps between events to stretch the battery for months.

    Uses: Wi-Fi, Sips power. Add: magnetic door switch, battery holder.

  • Garden shed sensor

    Send soil and air readings from the far end of the yard with long-range Bluetooth. A phone or hub picks them up.

    Uses: Bluetooth, Sips power. Add: soil moisture sensor, temperature sensor, battery holder.

  • Doorbell chime

    Play a short tune on a speaker each time the doorbell button is pressed. A message also lands on your phone.

    Uses: Wi-Fi, Makes sound. Add: audio amplifier board, small speaker, push button.

  • Classroom noise meter

    Measure sound with a digital microphone and show the level on a web page. A row of lights warns the class to quiet down.

    Uses: Hears sound, Wi-Fi. Add: digital microphone board, LED strip.

  • Internet radio

    Stream an online radio station over Wi-Fi and play it through a speaker. Buttons change the station.

    Uses: Wi-Fi, Makes sound. Add: audio amplifier board, speaker, push buttons.

  • Bird song detector

    Listen with a microphone near a feeder and log the loudest moments. Compare morning and evening activity.

    Uses: Hears sound, Wi-Fi. Add: digital microphone board.

  • Medicine reminder button

    Light a button at pill time and log each press online. Family members can see the dose was taken.

    Uses: Wi-Fi. Add: light-up push button.

  • CircuitPython classroom kit

    Load CircuitPython and teach students to read sensors with short scripts. Each student board posts results to a shared page.

    Uses: Wi-Fi. Add: temperature sensor, jumper wires.

Expert ideas

  • Wi-Fi 6 radio for a bigger chip

    Wire the chip to a larger processor and let it handle Wi-Fi 6 and Bluetooth. The bigger chip focuses on its own work.

    Uses: Wi-Fi, Bluetooth. Add: second processor board, jumper wires.
