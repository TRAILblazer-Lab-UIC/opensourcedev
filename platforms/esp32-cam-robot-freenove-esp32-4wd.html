Overview

The Freenove 4WD Car Kit for ESP32 is a four-wheel robot car you build yourself. An ESP32 board with a camera rides on a motor shield. It streams live video to a phone app over Wi-Fi. It suits hobbyists and teens who want a camera robot and are happy to follow a long tutorial.

Good for

  • Live video driving from a phone or laptop

  • Simple face detection with the Python PC client

Not great for

  • Quick starts, as assembly takes two to three hours
  • Buyers who want everything in the box, as the 18650 batteries are sold separately
  • Selling your own product, as the files are licensed for non-commercial use only
  • Smooth camera aim, as some buyers report jittery servos

Specs in plain words

  • The ESP32 runs at 240 MHz with two cores. It can drive motors and stream video at the same time.
  • 8 MB of extra memory holds camera pictures while they travel over Wi-Fi.
  • 4 MB of flash stores your program and its settings.
  • A motor chip on the shield drives all four motors and the servos. This leaves pins free for the camera.
  • The ultrasonic sensor measures distance with sound pulses, much like a bat.
  • Two 18650 cells power the car. Freenove asks for high-drain cells that give more than 3 amps.
Full spec sheet
ProcessorEspressif ESP32 (Xtensa LX6) in ESP32-WROVER-B module, 2 cores at 240 MHz
Memory520 KB plus 8 MB extra (PSRAM)
Storage4 MB
Ways to connect partsI2C, Serial (UART), USB device, Camera port (parallel)
RadiosWi-Fi, Bluetooth Low Energy, Bluetooth Classic
ConnectorsMicro USB, Camera connector, Servo ports, Motor ports, Battery plug, Pin headers
Built incamera, LED grid, color LED, light sensor, motor driver
Power2x 3.7 V 18650 Li-ion cells (not included, high-drain >3 A recommended)

Memory values are for the ESP32-WROVER-B module named in the kit datasheet folder (4 MB flash, 8 MB PSRAM). The ESP32 has 520 KB SRAM. The kit ships with one of two camera modules (OV2640 or GC0308 datasheets are in the repo), mounted the same way. Motor driving goes through PCA9685 channels 8 to 15. The tutorial does not name the H-bridge chip. The USB serial chip is a CH340, and drivers are in the repo. Car dimensions were not found.

Things you could build

15 ideas sized to what this board can do. Starter ideas suit first-time builders.

Starter ideas

  • Phone video driving

    Drive the car from the Freenove phone app over Wi-Fi. Watch the live camera view as you steer.

    Uses: Wi-Fi, Camera, Drives motors.

  • Obstacle dodger

    Upload the obstacle avoidance sketch. The car sweeps its ultrasonic head and turns away from walls.

    Uses: Drives motors, Sensors built in.

  • Line follower track

    Tape a black loop on the floor. The line tracking module keeps the car on the path.

    Uses: Drives motors, Sensors built in. Add: black electrical tape.

  • Flashlight chaser

    Shine a flashlight near the car. The two light sensors steer it toward the bright spot.

    Uses: Drives motors, Sensors built in. Add: flashlight.

  • Emoji face mood display

    Draw smiles, frowns, and hearts on the LED matrix face. Change the face to match how the car drives.

    Uses: Screen.

  • TV remote driver

    Steer the car with the included infrared remote. Map each button to a move or a light show.

    Uses: Drives motors.

  • Party light rover

    Program the color LEDs and buzzer to flash and beep in patterns. Let the car dance around the room.

    Uses: Drives motors.

Builder ideas

  • Pet watcher patrol

    Drive through the house from a laptop and check on a pet. The camera streams video over Wi-Fi.

    Uses: Wi-Fi, Camera, Drives motors.

  • Face detection greeter

    Run the Python PC client with face detection. The car notices a face and flashes its color LEDs.

    Uses: Wi-Fi, Camera.

  • Garden bed inspector

    Roll the car along a garden path and stream video. Spot pests and dry leaves without bending down.

    Uses: Wi-Fi, Camera, Drives motors, Battery ready.

  • Bluetooth gamepad control

    Write a sketch that reads commands over Bluetooth. Drive the car from a phone or a game controller app.

    Uses: Bluetooth, Drives motors.

  • Science fair speed test

    Time the car over a measured track at different motor speeds. Graph the results for a science project.

    Uses: Drives motors. Add: tape measure, stopwatch.

  • Snapshot nature cam

    Park the car near a bird feeder and grab a photo every few minutes. Save the images to a laptop over Wi-Fi.

    Uses: Wi-Fi, Camera, Battery ready.

Expert ideas

  • Room mapping explorer

    Combine ultrasonic distance readings with turns to sketch a rough room map. Send the points to a laptop.

    Uses: Wi-Fi, Sensors built in, Drives motors.

  • Color ball tracker

    Write a PC program that finds a red ball in the camera stream. Send steering commands back to the car.

    Uses: Wi-Fi, Camera, Drives motors. Add: red ball.

How open is it?

Open Board

Freenove shares its Arduino sketches, libraries, a Python PC client, and a schematic of the ESP32 board on GitHub. All of these files use the CC BY-NC-SA 3.0 license, which forbids commercial use. No board layout files were found, and the ESP32 Wi-Fi relies on closed Espressif code.
