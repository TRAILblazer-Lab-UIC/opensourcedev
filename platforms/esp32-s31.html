Espressif's high-end wireless chip for voice, touch screens, and smart home hubs.

Overview

The ESP32-S31 is Espressif's high-end wireless microcontroller. It has two RISC-V cores at up to 320 MHz. It packs Wi-Fi 6, Bluetooth, Thread, Zigbee, and a Gigabit Ethernet link into one chip. It suits experienced makers building voice gadgets, touch panels, and smart home hubs.

Good fit or not?

Good for

  • Voice gadgets and smart speakers
  • Touch screen control panels
  • Matter smart home hubs with Thread and Zigbee
  • Wired plus wireless sensor hubs
  • USB keyboards, music keyboards, and other USB add-ons

Not great for

  • Newcomers, with the chip and its tools still very new
  • MicroPython or CircuitPython users, with no builds yet
  • Large camera and screen projects better suited to the ESP32-P4
  • Shoppers who want many board choices today

Specs in plain words

  • Two 320 MHz cores can run a screen and handle sound at the same time.
  • 512 KB of memory plus 8 to 32 MB of PSRAM holds screen images, sound clips, and a web page.
  • 16 MB of flash on the main dev boards stores your program, fonts, and pictures.
  • 60 GPIO pins leave room for buttons, sensors, screens, and more.
  • Gigabit Ethernet gives a fast, steady wired network link.
  • 12 µA deep sleep lets a small low-power core keep watch while the rest rests.

Chip values come from the ESP32-S31 datasheet pre-release v0.6 (2026-11-20). It lists 512 KB HP SRAM, 32 KB LP SRAM, 320 KB ROM, 60 GPIO, two 12-bit SAR ADCs with up to 16 channels, a two-channel DAC and 12 µA typical deep sleep. The chip has no flash in the package. Variants NRV8, NRV16 and NRV32 differ only in PSRAM size. Flash and PSRAM shown are for the ESP32-S31-WROOM-3 N16R16 module used on the Function-CoreBoard-1, Korvo-1 and Waveshare 4.3C board. Connectors, onboard parts and size are for the Function-CoreBoard-1, which has two USB-C ports, a USB-A host port, an RJ45 jack and a 40-pin header. The datasheet is still marked preliminary.

Builder ideas

  • Talking kitchen timer

    Set a timer on a touch screen and hear a spoken alert at the end. Big buttons make it easy with messy hands.

    Uses: Screen, Makes sound. Add: touch screen, speaker.

  • Wired home sensor hub

    Plug the board into the router with a network cable and collect readings from wireless sensors. The wired link keeps the hub steady.

    Uses: Wired network, Thread or Zigbee. Add: network cable, wireless sensors.

  • Classroom noise sign

    Measure the room's sound level and show a colored face on a screen. Students see at a glance how loud they are.

    Uses: Hears sound, Screen. Add: small color screen.

  • Bird song recorder

    Record bird calls near a feeder to a memory card. Play them back later and try to name each species.

    Uses: Hears sound. Add: microSD card, microphone board.

  • Touch drum pad

    Turn the touch pins into drum pads made of foil. Tap a pad and hear a drum sound from the speaker.

    Uses: Makes sound. Add: aluminum foil, speaker.

  • USB keyboard notepad

    Plug a USB keyboard into the host port and type notes onto a screen. Notes save to a memory card.

    Uses: Plugs in USB gadgets, Screen. Add: USB keyboard, small screen, microSD card.

  • Medicine reminder screen

    Show pill times on a touch screen and log each tap as taken. Family members can check the log online.

    Uses: Screen, Wi-Fi. Add: touch screen.

  • Library visitor counter

    Count people passing a doorway with a motion sensor and post totals over the network. Staff see the busy hours.

    Uses: Wired network. Add: motion sensor, network cable.

Expert ideas

  • Matter smart home hub

    Bridge Thread and Zigbee sensors to your Wi-Fi network as a Matter gateway. Lights and door sensors from different brands work together.

    Uses: Wi-Fi, Thread or Zigbee. Add: Thread or Zigbee sensors.

  • Voice command light switch

    Listen for a wake word and a few short commands with the onboard microphone. Say a word and the lamp turns on.

    Uses: Hears sound, Wi-Fi. Add: relay module, lamp.

  • Smart speaker

    Combine microphones, a speaker, and Wi-Fi into a small voice assistant. It answers simple questions and plays music.

    Uses: Hears sound, Makes sound, Wi-Fi. Add: speaker, microphone board.

  • Video doorbell

    Snap a photo from a small camera each time the button is pressed. The picture lands on a screen inside the house.

    Uses: Camera, Wi-Fi, Screen. Add: camera module, push button, small screen.

  • USB MIDI music box

    Plug a USB music keyboard into the board and play synth sounds through a speaker. Kids can compose short tunes.

    Uses: Plugs in USB gadgets, Makes sound. Add: USB MIDI keyboard, speaker.

  • Greenhouse control panel

    Show temperature and soil moisture from wireless sensors on a wall screen. Tap a button to start the fans or water pump.

    Uses: Screen, Thread or Zigbee, Wi-Fi. Add: touch screen, wireless sensors, relay module, small water pump.

  • Car data logger

    Read messages from a car or e-bike CAN bus and save them to a memory card. Graph speed and battery use after a ride.

    Add: CAN transceiver board, microSD card.
