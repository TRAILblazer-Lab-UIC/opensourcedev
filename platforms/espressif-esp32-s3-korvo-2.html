Espressif Systems

Overview

The ESP32-S3-Korvo-2 is Espressif's audio and voice development board built on the ESP32-S3. It has two microphones, a speaker amplifier, a microSD slot, and a battery charger. It is the reference board for Espressif's ESP-ADF audio and ESP-SR speech libraries. It suits developers building smart speakers, wake word gadgets, and intercoms.

Good fit or not?

  • Echo-cancelled speakerphones and intercoms
  • Recording and playing sound on a memory card

Not great for

  • Buyers who expect a speaker, screen, or camera in the box
  • Buyers who need easy restocking, with several stores marking it discontinued
  • Projects that need many free pins for sensors

Specs in plain words

  • Two microphones let the board tell your voice apart from background noise.
  • An extra echo channel helps it ignore the sound from its own speaker.
  • A 3 W amplifier drives a small 4 ohm speaker loud enough for a room.
  • 8 MB of PSRAM holds sound buffers and small speech models.
  • 16 MB of flash stores programs, prompts, and short sound clips.
  • The battery socket and charger let it run without a wall plug.

Flash and PSRAM (16 MB and 8 MB) come from a search snippet of the V3.0 docs. The fetched V3.1 page names only the ESP32-S3-WROOM-1 module. The board has two microphones, an ES7210 ADC, an ES8311 codec and an NS4150 amplifier with a 2.00 mm speaker connector (4 ohm 3 W speaker recommended). No speaker is included. LEDs are a green standby LED, a red power and charging LED and two module-controlled LEDs (green and red). None is RGB. headers means the 2.54 mm test points for the codec, ADC, UART and I2C. Exposed GPIO count was not found. The docs say the battery charges over the Micro USB port. The USB connector type of the separate power port was not confirmed. The LCD extension board, camera, speaker and FPC cable are sold separately.

Things you could build

12 ideas sized to what this board can do. Starter ideas suit first-time builders.

Builder ideas

  • Internet radio

    Stream an online station over Wi-Fi and play it through a speaker. The PLAY and volume keys work as the controls.

    Uses: Wi-Fi, Makes sound. Add: 4 ohm speaker.

  • Memory card music player

    Play songs from a microSD card through a speaker. Use the onboard keys to skip tracks and change the volume.

    Uses: Makes sound. Add: microSD card, 4 ohm speaker.

  • Portable battery speaker

    Run the board from a Li-ion cell and stream music over Wi-Fi. Carry it to the garden or the garage.

    Uses: Battery ready, Makes sound, Wi-Fi. Add: Li-ion battery, 4 ohm speaker.

  • Voice memo recorder

    Press REC to save a spoken note to the memory card. Press PLAY to hear it again later.

    Uses: Hears sound, Makes sound. Add: microSD card, 4 ohm speaker.

  • Bird song recorder

    Record bird calls in the yard to a memory card on battery power. Compare the dawn chorus across the seasons.

    Uses: Hears sound, Battery ready. Add: microSD card, Li-ion battery.

  • Language practice parrot

    Record a word and play it back next to a teacher's recording. Students compare their pronunciation side by side.

    Uses: Hears sound, Makes sound. Add: microSD card, 4 ohm speaker.

  • Classroom noise meter

    Measure the sound level with the microphones and show it on the LCD board. The class sees when it gets too loud.

    Uses: Hears sound, Screen. Add: LCD extension board.

  • Talking photo frame

    Show pictures on the LCD board and play a recorded message with each one. It makes a sweet gift for a grandparent.

    Uses: Screen, Makes sound. Add: LCD extension board, microSD card, 4 ohm speaker.

Expert ideas

  • Wake word lamp

    Say a wake word and a short command to turn a lamp on or off. The ESP-SR library listens with the two microphones.

    Uses: Hears sound, Wi-Fi. Add: relay module, lamp.

  • Hands-free speakerphone

    Talk and listen through the board with echo cancellation. Your voice stays clear even with the speaker playing.

    Uses: Hears sound, Makes sound, Wi-Fi. Add: 4 ohm speaker.

  • Video intercom

    Add the camera and screen boards to make a two-way video intercom. Answer the front door from another room.

    Uses: Hears sound, Makes sound, Camera, Screen, Wi-Fi. Add: camera module, LCD extension board, 4 ohm speaker.

  • Voice controlled music box

    Say commands such as play, stop, or next to control songs. It shows how speech commands run on a small chip.

    Uses: Hears sound, Makes sound. Add: microSD card, 4 ohm speaker.

Espressif publishes schematic and board layout PDFs, with no hardware license stated. ESP-IDF and the board support package use Apache-2.0. ESP-ADF uses the Espressif MIT License, which limits it to Espressif chips. Parts of the speech libraries, the Wi-Fi and Bluetooth stacks, and the chip itself stay closed.
