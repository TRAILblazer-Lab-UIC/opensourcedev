Overview

The NanoPi R6S is a compact router and mini PC from FriendlyElec. It uses a Rockchip RK3588S chip with eight cores and a 6 TOPS AI helper. It has two 2.5 gigabit network ports and one Gigabit port. It suits home network tinkerers who also want a small media player or app server.

How the scores work

Good fit or not?

Good for

  • Fast home routing with FriendlyWrt or OpenWrt
  • A small file server or Docker host
  • Playing high resolution video on a TV
  • Light AI work with a USB camera

Not great for

  • Wireless projects without an extra USB adapter. Wi-Fi is not built in.
  • Projects with many sensor pins. It has only a small 12-pin connector.
  • Tight budgets. The bare board costs about 230 US dollars.
  • People who need a fully open board design

Specs in plain words

  • Eight processor cores handle routing, file sharing, and apps all at once.
  • 8 GB of memory runs a desktop Linux system with room to spare.
  • 6 TOPS means the AI helper does about 6 trillion small math steps each second.
  • 2.5 gigabit ports move data about two and a half times faster than a common home port.
  • HDMI 2.1 output can drive a TV at up to 8K.
  • The board is about 9 by 6 centimeters, close to the size of a deck of cards.

The wiki lists 32 GB eMMC. By contrast, the FriendlyElec shop now lists 8 GB RAM with 64 GB eMMC. flash_kb uses the wiki value. GPIO comes out on a 12-pin 0.5 mm FPC connector with up to 8 GPIOs, one SPI, three UARTs, one I2C and two SPDIF. USB is one USB 3.0 Type-A and one USB 2.0 Type-A. There is no Wi-Fi onboard. USB Wi-Fi adapters are supported. The board has an IR receiver, a user button, a MASK button and a HYM8563 RTC with a battery connector. The PCB is 8 layers.

How open is it?

Openness level

Level 2 of 3. Wild Open shares the most.

FriendlyElec publishes a schematic PDF and a DXF outline of the board. No board layout or Gerber files are public, and no hardware license is stated. FriendlyWrt and the Linux kernel are on GitHub, with the kernel under GPL-2.0. The Mali graphics driver, the NPU tools, and some Rockchip boot files stay closed.
