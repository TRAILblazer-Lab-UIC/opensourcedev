Overview

The Coral Dev Board Micro is a small board from Google for running AI right on the device. It pairs a fast NXP microcontroller with a Google Edge TPU chip for machine learning. A tiny camera and a microphone are built in. It can spot people or hear keywords right out of the box. Note that one distributor now lists it as scheduled for end of life.

Good fit or not?

Good for

  • Learning on-device vision and voice AI
  • Low-power person and keyword detection projects
  • Makers who want full board design files under an open license
  • Arduino users who want to try machine learning

Not great for

  • Photos or video, as the camera is only 324 by 324 pixels
  • Common 3.3 V and 5 V sensors, which need level shifters
  • Windows users of the C++ SDK, which builds only on Linux and macOS
  • Long-term projects, given the end-of-life warning

Specs in plain words

  • An 800 MHz main core plus a 400 MHz helper core make it fast for a microcontroller.
  • The Edge TPU does 4 trillion simple math steps per second for AI models.
  • 64 MB of SDRAM holds images and small AI models while they run.
  • 128 MiB of flash stores your program and models when power is off.
  • The 1.8 V pins drive only about 6 mA, enough for an LED but not much more.
  • The board is 65 by 30 mm and weighs about 10 grams, close to a stick of gum.
Full spec sheet
ProcessorNXP i.MX RT1176 (Cortex-M7 800 MHz plus Cortex-M4 400 MHz) with Google Edge TPU, 2 cores at 800 MHz
Memory2 MB plus 64 MB extra (PSRAM)

AI chipGoogle Edge TPU 4 TOPS
Pins3 analog inputs
Ways to connect partsI2C, SPI, Serial (UART), Digital audio (I2S), Digital microphone (PDM), USB device, Camera port (MIPI CSI), Display port (MIPI DSI), Ethernet, SD card bus, CAN bus, Debug port
ConnectorsUSB-C, Pin headers
Built incamera, microphone, buttons
Power5 V over USB-C (5 V 2 A typical)
Size65 x 30 mm

ram_kb is the 2 MB on-chip SRAM. psram_kb holds the 64 MB external SDRAM. flash_kb is the 128 MiB NAND. The two 12-pin headers carry GPIO, PWM, UART, I2C, SPI, DAC, ADC and power pins. As a result, the plain GPIO count is not given as one number. I/O is 1.8 V. The datasheet lists three 12-bit ADC inputs, two of them on the header. MIPI CSI and DSI, Ethernet RGMII, SDIO and most audio lanes are only on the board-to-board connectors. Ethernet needs the PoE Add-on, and Wi-Fi and Bluetooth need the Wireless Add-on. Board thickness is 6.8 mm and weight 10.4 g. Edge TPU power peaks around 3 W.

Things you could build

13 ideas sized to what this board can do. Starter ideas suit first-time builders.

Starter ideas

  • Person detector demo

    Flash the built-in person detection example and watch the LED react. It is a quick first taste of on-device AI.

    Uses: Camera, AI chip, Sensors built in.

  • Face detection badge

    Light an LED each time the camera sees a face. Use it as a fun science fair demo on how AI spots patterns.

    Uses: Camera, AI chip, Sensors built in.

Builder ideas

  • Wake word light switch

    Train the board to hear a keyword with its microphone. Say the word and a relay turns on a lamp.

    Uses: Hears sound, AI chip, Sensors built in. Add: relay module, level shifter.

  • Room occupancy counter

    Count people as they pass the camera. Teachers can see how busy a room gets during the day.

    Uses: Camera, AI chip.

  • Bird feeder visitor alert

    Detect visitors at a feeder with a small image model. Log each visit for a nature journal.

    Uses: Camera, AI chip. Add: weatherproof box, USB power bank.

  • Clap and sound sorter

    Classify claps, whistles, and knocks with a small audio model. Each sound triggers a different action.

    Uses: Hears sound, AI chip.

  • Quiet zone noise meter

    Measure sound level with the onboard microphone. Blink a warning light in a library when it gets loud.

    Uses: Hears sound, Sensors built in. Add: LED strip, level shifter.

Expert ideas

  • Hand gesture game controller

    Teach the camera a few hand shapes. Use them as buttons in a simple computer game.

    Uses: Camera, AI chip.

  • Plant health watcher

    Snap a photo of a houseplant every hour and check leaf color with a model. Get a hint when it needs care.

    Uses: Camera, AI chip.

  • Talking doorbell

    Detect a person at the door and play a greeting through a small speaker. It needs an audio add-on board.

    Uses: Camera, AI chip, Makes sound. Add: audio amplifier add-on, small speaker.

  • Networked smart camera

    Add the PoE Add-on and send detection events over Ethernet. One cable carries both power and data.

    Uses: Wired network, Camera, AI chip. Add: PoE Add-on board, PoE network switch.

  • Tiny screen AI viewer

    Show what the camera sees and its guesses on a small display. Students can watch the model think.

    Uses: Screen, Camera, AI chip. Add: small MIPI display, custom add-on board.

  • Custom add-on board design

    Start from Google's KiCad template and design your own add-on board. Plug it into the two big connectors.

    Add: custom printed circuit board.
