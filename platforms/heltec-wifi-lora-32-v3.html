Overview

The Heltec WiFi LoRa 32 V3 is a small board with an ESP32-S3 chip, a LoRa long-range radio, Wi-Fi, and Bluetooth. It also has a tiny screen and a battery charger. It is one of the cheapest and most common boards for Meshtastic, the open mesh texting system. Hobbyists also program it with Arduino tools for long-range sensor projects.

  • Swapping the Wi-Fi antenna, as it is soldered on

  • Folks who want a finished case in the box

Specs in plain words

  • The ESP32-S3 has two cores at 240 MHz. It can run the radio, the screen, and Bluetooth together.
  • 8 MB of flash storage holds the Meshtastic firmware with room to spare.
  • The LoRa radio sends short messages for miles across open ground. Buy the band your country allows, such as 915 MHz in the US or 868 MHz in Europe.
  • The 0.96 inch screen is about the size of a postage stamp. It shows messages, node names, and battery level.
  • Deep sleep draws about 15 millionths of an amp. A battery can last a long time between short wake-ups.
  • The whole board is about 5 cm long and 2.5 cm wide, close to a stick of gum.

The ESP32-S3FN8 has 8 MB flash in the package and no PSRAM. The GPIO count is the 28 GPIO-numbered pins on the two 18-pin headers. Several are shared with the OLED, LED, Vext control and battery sense. ADC is 7 ADC1 plus 2 ADC2 channels per the datasheet. Sleep current is 15 uA on battery and 10 uA when powered from the 3.3 V header (datasheet V3.2). TX power is 21 +/- 1 dBm. The LF version covers 470-510 MHz and the HF version covers 863-928 MHz. The USB-C port goes through a CP2102 bridge. As a result, native ESP32-S3 USB is not wired by default. V3.1 and V3.2 are minor revisions that run the same firmware.

Starter ideas

  • Off-grid chat for camping trips

    Give each camper a board paired with their phone. Everyone can text the group in places with no cell signal.

    Uses: LoRa long range radio, Mesh messaging, Bluetooth. Add: small rechargeable battery.

  • Neighborhood emergency mesh

    Keep a node in each home on your street. Neighbors can post a quick check-in during a power or phone outage.

    Uses: LoRa long range radio, Mesh messaging, Bluetooth. Add: USB power adapter.

  • Window repeater

    Park a node high in a window on USB power. It passes along messages for radios across the block.

    Uses: LoRa long range radio, Mesh messaging. Add: USB power adapter.

  • Classroom radio range test

    Students carry nodes across the school grounds and note where messages still arrive. They learn how walls and hills block radio waves.

    Uses: LoRa long range radio, Mesh messaging.

  • Pocket message pager

    Add a small battery and a printed case. Incoming texts pop up on the little screen without your phone.

    Uses: LoRa long range radio, Mesh messaging, Screen, Battery ready. Add: small rechargeable battery, small case.

Builder ideas

  • Garden soil moisture alerts

    Wire a soil sensor to the board and send readings across the yard by radio. A second board indoors shows which beds need water.

    Uses: LoRa long range radio, Battery ready, Sips power. Add: soil moisture sensor, small rechargeable battery, weatherproof box.

  • Mailbox opened alert

    Fit a magnetic door switch to a far-off mailbox. The board sends a radio ping each time the lid opens.

    Uses: LoRa long range radio, Sips power, Battery ready. Add: magnetic door switch, small rechargeable battery.

  • Backyard weather station

    Add a temperature and humidity sensor. The screen shows the reading and the radio shares it with the house.

    Uses: LoRa long range radio, Screen. Add: temperature and humidity sensor, weatherproof box.

  • Barn or greenhouse watcher

    Place a board with a temperature sensor out in a far shed. It warns a node in the house about frost at night.

    Uses: LoRa long range radio, Battery ready. Add: temperature sensor, small rechargeable battery.

  • Wildlife trail visit counter

    Point a motion sensor at an animal path. The board counts visits and radios a daily total back home.

    Uses: LoRa long range radio, Sips power, Battery ready. Add: motion sensor, small rechargeable battery, weatherproof box.

  • Two-way doorbell for a big yard

    Press a button at the gate and a buzzer sounds at the house. A second button sends a reply back.

    Uses: LoRa long range radio. Add: push button, small buzzer.

  • Treasure hunt radio beacons

    Hide boards around a park that each send a short clue. Players read the clues on their own board's screen.

    Uses: LoRa long range radio, Screen, Battery ready. Add: small rechargeable battery.

Expert ideas

  • Wi-Fi bridge for sensor data

    Let a home board pass LoRa readings onto your Wi-Fi network. You can chart garden or barn data on a computer.

    Uses: LoRa long range radio, Wi-Fi. Add: USB power adapter.

  • LoRaWAN sensor node

    Use the open RadioLib library to join a community LoRaWAN network. Your sensor data shows up online from miles away.

    Uses: LoRa long range radio, Sips power. Add: temperature sensor.

  • Science club signal logger

    Log signal strength from a distant node every minute. Students graph how weather and time of day change the range.

    Uses: LoRa long range radio, Wi-Fi.

How open is it?

Open Board

Heltec shares schematic PDFs, pin maps, and 3D files. No board layout files are posted, and Heltec allows only personal, non-commercial use of its documents. The Meshtastic firmware is open under GPL-3.0, and the Arduino tools are open. The ESP32-S3 chip and its Wi-Fi and Bluetooth drivers stay closed.
