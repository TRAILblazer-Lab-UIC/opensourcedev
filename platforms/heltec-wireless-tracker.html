Heltec Wireless Tracker

Heltec Automation

An ESP32-S3 board with LoRa radio, GPS, and a small color screen.

Overview

The Heltec Wireless Tracker is an ESP32-S3 board with a LoRa long-range radio, a GPS receiver and a 0.96 inch color screen. It also has Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, and a battery charger. People use it as a Meshtastic node with location sharing or as a portable position beacon. Heltec now sells V2, which adds a stronger radio and a solar input.

Good fit or not?

Good for

  • Texting and sharing GPS spots off the grid with Meshtastic
  • Tracking a bike, a pet, or a walk
  • Learning LoRa, GPS, and Wi-Fi in Arduino
  • Portable projects with a built-in screen

Not great for

  • Long battery life with GPS on, as the receiver draws a lot of power
  • Projects with many extra parts, as the screen and radios use many pins
  • Big memory jobs, as the board has no extra PSRAM
  • Commercial copies, as Heltec forbids commercial use of its files

Specs in plain words

  • The ESP32-S3 runs at 240 MHz with two cores. It can track GPS, run the radio, and drive the screen together.
  • 8 MB of flash storage holds the firmware with room for logs and settings.
  • The LoRa radio sends short messages for miles across open ground. Buy the band your country allows, such as 915 MHz in the US or 868 MHz in Europe.
  • V2 can transmit at up to 28 dBm. Check your local radio rules and set the power to match.
  • The GPS listens to several satellite systems. It needs a clear view of the sky.
  • The screen is about the size of a postage stamp. It shows messages, position, and battery level.

Size53 x 25.4 mm

Values describe V2, the version Heltec sells now. V1.1 shares the chip set but measures 65.48 x 28.06 mm and transmits at 21 dBm. The ESP32-S3FN8 has 8 MB flash in the package and no PSRAM, which the LaskaKit and Zephyr pages also show. The ADC count is 7 ADC1 plus 2 ADC2 channels from the V1.1 product page. Both versions have two 20-pin headers per the V2 datasheet (the V1.1 page says 18-pin), and many pins drive the TFT, GNSS and LoRa. Sleep is 21 uA on battery per the V2 datasheet. GNSS draws about 127 mA. V2 uses a CN3165 charger. V1.1 uses a TP4054 per its schematic.

Openness level

Level 2 of 3. Wild Open shares the most.

Heltec shares schematic PDFs and mechanical drawings for the board. No layout files are posted, and Heltec's documents forbid commercial reuse. The Heltec ESP32 library is open under MIT, and Meshtastic is open under GPL-3.0. The Wi-Fi and Bluetooth drivers and Heltec's LoRaWAN library stay closed.
