Overview

The Home Assistant Connect ZBT-1 is a small USB radio stick for a Home Assistant server. It lets that server talk to Zigbee or Thread smart home devices such as bulbs, plugs and sensors. It first sold in 2022 as the SkyConnect and got a new name in 2024. Production ended in 2025, and Nabu Casa now points buyers to the Connect ZBT-2.

Good fit or not?

Good for

  • Adding Zigbee lights and sensors to Home Assistant
  • Running Thread and Matter devices from Home Assistant
  • Keeping a smart home local with no cloud account
  • Easy firmware updates from inside Home Assistant

Not great for

  • New buyers, as stores have mostly sold out
  • Writing your own programs, as it has no free pins
  • Running Zigbee and Thread together, as that firmware is experimental
  • Plugging straight into a USB 3 port, which adds radio noise

Specs in plain words

  • The EFR32MG21 radio chip speaks Zigbee or Thread at 2.4 GHz. You pick one by loading its firmware.
  • Up to 20 dBm of sending power reaches across a typical home. Mains-powered devices relay the signal further.
  • 512 KB of flash holds one radio firmware at a time.
  • The stick is about 4 cm long and weighs 4 grams. That is about the size of a small thumb drive.
  • It draws up to 150 milliamps from USB. That is a light load for a normal USB port.

The exact chip part EFR32MG21A020F512IM32 comes from Nabu Casa's firmware manifest. Flash and RAM come from the Silicon Labs SDK device header. CNX Software lists the MG21 family maximum of 1024 KB flash and 96 KB RAM, which does not apply to this part. The radio talks to the host over UART through a CP2102N USB bridge. No user GPIO is exposed. Height is 4.5 mm and weight is 4 g. Matter works through Thread with Home Assistant acting as the Matter controller.

The firmware build scripts are open under Apache-2.0, and the flasher tool is open under GPL-3.0. Home Assistant itself is open source. No schematics or board files were published for this stick, and the radio chip and some Silicon Labs build tools stay closed.
