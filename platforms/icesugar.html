Overview

The iCESugar is a low-cost board built around a Lattice iCE40 FPGA. An FPGA is a chip you rewire with code to become almost any digital circuit. A helper chip on the board shows up as a USB drive, and you program the FPGA by dragging a file onto it. It suits hobbyists on a budget who want to learn Verilog with fully open tools.

Specs in plain words

  • 5280 logic cells is room for a small RISC-V processor plus a few extra circuits.
  • 128 KB of fast memory holds a short program for a soft processor.
  • 8 MB of flash stores your design and extra data such as sounds or pictures.
  • 8 math blocks speed up multiplying numbers for sound and signal projects.
  • A 12 MHz clock comes from the helper chip. A bad pin setting cannot easily brick the board.

ram_kb is the 1 Mbit single-port RAM. The FPGA also has 128 Kbit of dual-port block RAM. clock_mhz is the 12 MHz clock output by the iCELink STM32 on its MCO pin. The FPGA also has 48 MHz and 10 kHz internal oscillators and a PLL. flash_kb is the W25Q64 listed as 8 MB in the README and on Tindie. A W25Q64 is a 64 Mbit (8 MB) part. fpgadeveloper lists "8 Mb", likely a typo. The GPIO count was not stated. fpgadeveloper lists 4 Pmod connectors. The FPGA has two hard SPI and two hard I2C blocks. JTAG is for debugging SoCs in the FPGA.

Things you could build

11 ideas sized to what this board can do. Starter ideas suit first-time builders.

Starter ideas

  • Rainbow LED blinker

    Write a first Verilog design that cycles the RGB LED through colors. Drag the file onto the board and watch it glow.

  • Switch and LED logic lab

    Turn the DIP switch into inputs for logic gates you design. Students see each gate light the LED in a different color.

  • Button press counter

    Count button presses on a number display. It is a classic first step into counters and timers.

    Add: seven segment display add-on.

Builder ideas

  • Hello over the serial port

    Build a simple serial sender in Verilog. Read your message in a terminal on your computer.

  • Reaction time game

    Flash the LED at a random moment and time the button press. Show the score on a small number display.

    Add: seven segment display add-on.

  • Tone and melody maker

    Make square wave tones and play a short tune through a small speaker. Each switch can pick a different note.

    Add: small speaker, small amplifier board.

  • Servo sweep for a puppet

    Make a precise pulse that swings a servo arm. Use it to wave a puppet hand in an art project.

    Add: servo motor, jumper wires.

  • LED strip light show

    Drive a strip of color LEDs with patterns you design in logic. Hang it in a classroom window.

    Add: LED strip, 5 V power supply.

  • Frequency counter for science class

    Measure how fast a signal flips on and off. Test a buzzer, a fan sensor or a signal generator.

    Add: jumper wires.

Expert ideas

  • Build a RISC-V computer

    Load a small RISC-V soft core and turn the FPGA into a tiny computer. Then run short C programs on it.

  • Weather readings on a soft core

    Read a temperature and humidity sensor through the I2C pins. Send the readings to your computer every minute.

    Add: temperature and humidity sensor.

How open is it?

Open Board

MuseLab publishes the board schematic as a PDF, but no layout files or license. The FPGA tools and the icesprog flasher are open. The iCELink firmware ships only as a binary, and the iCE40 chip is closed.
