Overview

The Jetson Orin Nano Super Developer Kit is a small computer from NVIDIA made for AI. It has a graphics chip with over a thousand cores that runs vision and language models on the board. It runs JetPack, which is Ubuntu Linux plus NVIDIA's AI tools. It suits students, robot builders, and tinkerers who already know some Linux and Python.

Good fit or not?

Good for

  • Running AI on live camera video
  • Local chat and voice models with no cloud

  • Learning the CUDA and AI tools used in industry

Not great for

  • Battery gadgets or tiny low-power sensors
  • Tight budgets
  • Screens that only take HDMI with no adapter

Specs in plain words

  • 67 TOPS means it can do 67 trillion small math steps each second for AI.
  • 8 GB of fast memory holds a small language model or several vision models.
  • The six-core 1.7 GHz processor runs Ubuntu Linux at desktop speed.
  • Two camera connectors let you watch from two angles at once.
  • It draws 7 to 25 watts, about as much as a small desk lamp.
  • The board is about 10 by 9 centimeters, roughly the size of a paperback book cover.
Full spec sheet
Processor6-core Arm Cortex-A78AE v8.2 (Jetson Orin Nano 8GB module), 6 cores at 1.7 GHz
Memory8 GB
StoragemicroSD card
AI chipNVIDIA Ampere GPU, 1024 CUDA cores and 32 tensor cores 67 TOPS
Ways to connect partsI2C, SPI, Serial (UART), Digital audio (I2S), USB host, USB device, Camera port (MIPI CSI), PCIe, Ethernet
RadiosWi-Fi, Ethernet
ConnectorsUSB-A, USB-C, Pin headers, Camera connector, M.2 slot, microSD slot
Power19 V DC barrel jack (5.5 x 2.5 mm), 7 to 25 W
Size103 x 90.5 mm

The 1.7 GHz clock and 67 TOPS apply in the Super (MAXN SUPER) power mode. There is no onboard eMMC. Storage is a microSD card on the module or an NVMe SSD on the carrier. Display output is one DisplayPort 1.2 connector. HDMI and video over USB-C are not supported. The included M.2 Key E card gives 802.11ac Wi-Fi. Bluetooth was not confirmed. Height is 34.77 mm with module and heatsink.
