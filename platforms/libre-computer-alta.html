Libre Computer Alta

Libre Computer (Shenzhen Libre Technology)

A Pi-sized Linux board with a 5 TOPS AI chip for camera projects.

Field marks: Look for a Raspberry Pi 3 lookalike with an AI chip, four USB ports, zero Wi-Fi, and a software support promise running to 2053.

Overview

Alta is a single-board computer from Libre Computer the size of a Raspberry Pi 3. It runs an Amlogic A311D chip with six cores and a 5 TOPS AI accelerator. Its AI chip works with the regular open Mesa graphics stack, which is rare for small boards. It suits makers who want camera and AI projects on standard Linux.

Good fit or not?

Good for

  • Camera projects with on-board AI
  • Running standard Linux images through UEFI boot
  • Wired network servers with Power over Ethernet
  • Media players and digital signs on HDMI 2.0

Not great for

  • Wireless projects, with no Wi-Fi or Bluetooth on board
  • Beginners, with docs spread across forum threads
  • Easy buying, with one listed store and no confirmed US shop
  • Tiny battery gadgets, with a typical draw of about 7.5 watts

Specs in plain words

  • Six cores at up to 2 GHz feel like a capable budget desktop.
  • A 5 TOPS AI chip can spot objects in live camera video.
  • 4 GB of memory runs a desktop, a browser, and an AI model together.
  • Camera and display ports match the Raspberry Pi layout. Many Pi add-ons fit.
  • Four USB 3.0 ports move files from external drives quickly.
  • It uses under 1.5 watts idle and about 10 watts at full load.

RAM is the common 4 GB model. A 2 GB model is also listed. The clock is the 2.016 GHz figure from the official spec page. CNX Software lists 2.2 GHz for the A311D. Storage is microSD or an optional eMMC module. As a result, flash_kb is null. The 16 MB SPI NOR holds the UEFI firmware. The 40-pin header carries PWM, 3x I2C, 4x UART, PCM, SPI, SPDIF, TDM and JTAG. The usable GPIO count was not found. Power is under 1.5 W idle, 7.5 W typical and 10 W full load. Height is 20 mm. There is an IR receiver and a USB ROM boot button. There is no Wi-Fi or Bluetooth.
