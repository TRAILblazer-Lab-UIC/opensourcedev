Overview

The LILYGO T-Camera S3 is a small camera board built on the ESP32-S3 chip. It packs a 2 MP camera, a tiny OLED screen, a motion sensor, and a microphone onto one stick. Wi-Fi, Bluetooth LE, and a battery charger are built in. It suits makers who want a motion-triggered camera without wiring extra parts.

Good fit or not?

Good for

  • Wi-Fi cameras that wake on motion
  • Smart home cameras through ESPHome
  • QR code readers and small vision experiments
  • Sound detection with the built-in microphone
  • Battery camera projects in its optional shell

Not great for

  • Recording long videos, as it has no microSD slot
  • Complete beginners, as setup has a few traps
  • Projects that need many free pins

Specs in plain words

  • A 240 MHz dual-core chip handles a live camera stream over Wi-Fi.
  • 8 MB of PSRAM holds a few full camera frames at once.
  • 16 MB of flash stores your program and many small images.
  • The 0.96 inch screen is about the size of a fingernail and shows short text or icons.
  • The motion sensor notices warm bodies moving nearby and can wake the board.
  • The board is about 69 by 28 millimeters, close to a stick of gum with a dome on top.

Size69 x 28 x 18.5 mm

Size is from CNX Software and includes the PIR dome. The optional shell is 75 x 35 x 12 mm. The README lists free GPIO 3, 15, 16, 45 and 46 among others. CNX describes a 5-pin connector (IO15, IO16, 5V, 3.3V, GND), a 5-pin header with 3 GPIO and a 6-pin header with UART and 4 GPIO. The exact free GPIO count is unclear. As a result, gpio is null. The v1.6 board has an I2S microphone and the v1.7 board has a PDM microphone. The onboard list has no motion sensor value for PIR. The AS312 PIR sensor sits on GPIO17 per the README.

How open is it?

Openness level

Level 2 of 3. Wild Open shares the most.

LILYGO publishes schematic PDFs and an outline drawing on GitHub. No layout files or license come with them. The hardware counts as closed for that reason. Arduino, ESP-IDF, and ESPHome are open code tools. The ESP32-S3 Wi-Fi and Bluetooth drivers stay closed.
