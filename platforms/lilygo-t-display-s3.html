Overview

The LilyGO T-Display S3 is a small ESP32-S3 board with a 1.9 inch color screen on its face. It also has Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, two buttons, and a battery charger. It costs about 9 US dollars from LilyGO. Hobbyists use it to build clocks, dashboards, music tools, and small games.

Specs in plain words

  • The 1.9 inch screen is about the size of a stick of gum and shows 170 by 320 color dots.
  • A 240 MHz dual-core chip handles a busy screen and Wi-Fi together.
  • 8 MB of extra memory holds pictures, fonts, and game data.
  • 16 MB of storage leaves plenty of room for big programs.
  • The battery plug charges a small rechargeable battery from USB-C.
  • The board is about 62 by 26 millimeters, about the length of a finger.

GPIO and ADC counts are from CNX Software and refer to pins free on the two 12-pin headers. A 4-pin connector adds 3.3 V, GND and two GPIO for I2C or UART. The LCD uses GPIO 38 to 48 and GPIO 5 to 9. As a result, many ESP32-S3 pins are taken. GPIO15 must be set high to power the LCD on battery. Interfaces listed are those of the ESP32-S3 that are reachable on this board. The SD pins in the pinout are only for an SD shield add-on. The touch version adds a CST816 touch controller on I2C.

Starter ideas

  • Internet desk clock

    Fetch the exact time and weather over Wi-Fi and show them in color. It sits neatly beside a keyboard.

    Uses: Wi-Fi, Screen.

  • Pocket stopwatch and timer

    Use the two buttons to start and stop a timer on the screen. Clip it to a bag for sports practice.

    Uses: Screen, Battery ready. Add: small rechargeable battery.

  • Name badge that scrolls

    Show your name and a fun message in scrolling color text. Wear it at a meetup or a school fair.

    Uses: Screen, Battery ready. Add: small rechargeable battery, lanyard.

  • Battery level checker

    Read the battery voltage pin and draw a battery icon on screen. Learn how gadgets track their charge.

    Uses: Battery ready, Screen. Add: small rechargeable battery.

Builder ideas

  • Home dashboard with ESPHome

    Show room temperatures and light states from your smart home. The screen updates as things change.

    Uses: Wi-Fi, Screen.

  • MIDI piano visualizer

    Plug the board into a computer as a USB music device and light up notes on the screen as songs play. It helps young players see each key.

    Uses: Screen.

  • Plant care monitor

    Read a soil sensor and draw a moisture gauge on the screen. A web page shows the same reading on your phone.

    Uses: Wi-Fi, Screen. Add: soil moisture sensor.

  • Garden weather station

    Read temperature, humidity, and air pressure from a small sensor. Show trends on the screen and post them online.

    Uses: Wi-Fi, Screen. Add: weather sensor board.

  • Sound level meter

    Add a digital microphone and show a bouncing volume bar. Use it to keep a classroom calm.

    Uses: Hears sound, Screen. Add: digital microphone board.

  • Tiny music player

    Play sound clips through a small amplifier and speaker. The screen shows the track name.

    Uses: Makes sound, Screen. Add: audio amplifier board, small speaker.

  • Bluetooth step tracker screen

    Read a motion sensor and count steps through the day. Send the total to a phone over Bluetooth.

    Uses: Bluetooth, Screen, Battery ready. Add: motion sensor board, small rechargeable battery.

  • Touch menu for a smart lamp

    Use the touch version to pick lamp colors with a finger. The board sends the color to an LED strip.

    Uses: Screen. Add: LED strip, 5 volt power supply.

  • Bus and train arrival sign

    Fetch live transit times over Wi-Fi and list the next departures. Hang it by the front door.

    Uses: Wi-Fi, Screen.

  • Science data logger

    Read a temperature probe every few seconds and draw a live chart. Students watch ice melt or water heat up.

    Uses: Screen. Add: waterproof temperature probe.

Expert ideas

  • Handheld retro game

    Run the NES emulator example and play classic games on the color screen. Add buttons for a tiny handheld.

    Uses: Screen, Battery ready. Add: push buttons, small rechargeable battery.

    Design a full touch interface with menus and sliders using LVGL. Build a control panel for any project.
