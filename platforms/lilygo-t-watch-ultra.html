LILYGO T-Watch Ultra

LILYGO (Xinyuan)

A hackable ESP32-S3 smartwatch with LoRa, GPS, NFC, and an AMOLED touchscreen.

Overview

The LILYGO T-Watch Ultra is a programmable smartwatch built on the ESP32-S3 chip. It has a bright 2.06 inch AMOLED touchscreen in a dust and splash resistant case. Inside sit Wi-Fi, Bluetooth LE, a LoRa radio, GPS, and an NFC reader. It suits makers and hikers who want a wrist gadget they can reprogram or turn into a Meshtastic messenger.

Good fit or not?

Good for

  • Off-grid texting with Meshtastic
  • GPS tracking on hikes and field trips
  • Custom watch faces and wrist apps
  • Motion and step experiments with a smart sensor
  • NFC tag projects around the house

Not great for

  • Adding your own sensors, as every chip pin already drives a part inside
  • Beginners on the Arduino path, as it needs an alpha core and two libraries
  • Tight budgets, at about 90 USD
  • Fast charging, as charge current must stay under 500 mA

Specs in plain words

  • A 240 MHz dual-core chip runs a smooth touch interface and radio tasks together.
  • 8 MB of PSRAM is room for full-screen images on the 410 by 502 pixel display.
  • The LoRa radio sends short texts across long distances with no phone towers.
  • The GPS receiver finds your spot on a map outdoors.
  • The 1100 mAh battery sits inside and charges over USB-C.
  • The case is about 63 by 49 millimeters, close to a matchbox on your wrist.

All ESP32-S3 pins drive internal parts, and the LilyGoLib hardware doc marks none as free. The low_power_ua value is deep sleep with button wake and backup power off. Power off with backup only draws 77 uA. LILYGO lists a 2.06 inch display. By contrast, CNX Software reported 2.01 inch. The IP65 rating comes from CNX Software, and the LILYGO wiki does not list it. The board has a MAX98357A amplifier for a speaker, but no speaker part was confirmed in the docs. The LILYGO wiki also lists CC1101, LR1121 and SI4432 radio options that are not sold in the store. The USB-C port charges and programs the watch but cannot power external devices.
