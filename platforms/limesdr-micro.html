Overview

The LimeSDR Micro is a software-defined radio on a small M.2 card. It plugs into a computer and turns software into a radio that can listen and transmit. It tunes from 30 MHz to 3.8 GHz and has a built-in signal processor. It suits radio hobbyists, researchers, and private 4G and 5G network labs.

  • Tight budgets, at about 249 USD

Specs in plain words

  • A tuning range of 30 MHz to 3.8 GHz covers FM radio, aircraft signals, Wi-Fi, and many phone bands.
  • Up to 100 MHz of bandwidth lets it watch a wide slice of the airwaves at once.
  • One transmit and two receive channels let it talk on one path and listen on two.
  • The signal processor handles up to 80 GFLOPs of math on the card itself.
  • The M.2 2280 card is about 80 by 22 millimeters, close to a stick of gum.
  • PCIe Gen 3 links the card to the host for fast data.

The cores and clock describe the LA9310 Arm Cortex-M4 control core. The card has a 512 Kbit EEPROM for initial configuration, and no RAM or flash size was found. The gpio value is the four 3.3 V GPIOs on the 8-pin FPC expansion connector, which also carries I2C. RF ports are three MHF4 connectors for TX, RX1 and RX2, plus four MHF4 connectors for clock and PPS. Components stand about 4 mm above the top side, more than standard M.2. As a result, it may not fit laptops. An optional GNSS version adds an Antenova M10578-A3 receiver. Specs come from the CNX Software article that summarizes the Crowd Supply page, which blocked direct fetching.

Things you could build

Builder ideas

  • FM radio on a computer

    Tune in local FM stations with a radio app on the host computer. Watch the stations appear as peaks on a live chart.

    Add: antenna, host computer with M.2 slot.

  • Radio spectrum explorer

    Sweep from 30 MHz to 3.8 GHz and see which bands are busy. Learn where Wi-Fi, phones, and TV signals live.

    Add: wideband antenna, host computer with M.2 slot.

  • Airplane tracker

    Pick up position messages that airliners broadcast. Plot nearby flights on a map at home or at school.

    Add: 1090 MHz antenna, host computer with M.2 slot.

  • Wireless signal classroom demo

    Show students how a radio wave carries sound and data. Draw live signal shapes on a projector.

    Add: antenna, projector.

  • Card health monitor

    Read the onboard temperature sensor from a script. Log heat over a long radio session.

    Uses: Sensors built in.

Expert ideas

  • Weather satellite pictures

    Catch signals from passing weather satellites. Turn them into cloud pictures of your region.

    Add: satellite antenna, host computer with M.2 slot.

  • Private 4G network lab

    Run open cellular software such as srsRAN on a host computer. Test phones on a tiny private network in a shielded lab.

    Add: test phone, test SIM cards, shielded box.

  • Private 5G research cell

    Use OpenAirInterface to build a small 5G cell for a university lab. Students study how mobile networks work.

    Add: host computer with M.2 slot, antennas, test phone.

  • Ham radio transceiver

    Licensed amateur radio operators can build a digital radio in software. Talk to other hams on the bands the card covers.

    Add: antenna, amateur radio license.

  • Handheld radio scanner

    Fit the card into a small handheld computer with an M.2 slot. Carry a pocket radio scanner on walks.

    Add: handheld computer with M.2 slot, antenna.

  • Radio direction finder

    Use the two receive channels to compare one signal at two antennas. Estimate which way a transmitter lies.

    Add: two matched antennas.

Lime Microsystems publishes the full Altium schematics, layouts, Gerbers, and parts list under the Solderpad Hardware License v2.1. The driver LimeSuiteNG is Apache-2.0, and the DSP firmware is BSD-3-Clause. The DSP compiler appears closed, and the LA9310 and LMS7002M chips are closed silicon.
