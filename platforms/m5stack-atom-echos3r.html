Overview

The Atom VoiceS3R is a 24 mm cube from M5Stack with a speaker, a microphone, and an ESP32-S3 chip. It was first sold as the Atom EchoS3R and was renamed in April 2026. It makes a cheap voice satellite for Home Assistant or a small AI voice assistant. Newcomers can use ready firmware with no code at all.

Good fit or not?

Good for

  • Adding voice control to a Home Assistant smart home
  • Trying an AI voice assistant on a small budget
  • Small speaker and microphone projects
  • Learning Arduino, MicroPython, or blocks with UiFlow2

Not great for

  • Hearing voices from across a large room, with only one microphone
  • Loud music, as the speaker is 1 W
  • Projects that need many pins, with only two free on the expansion port

Specs in plain words

  • The ESP32-S3 has two cores at 240 MHz, plenty for listening and talking back.
  • 8 MB of PSRAM gives room for wake word detection on the device.
  • 8 MB of flash stores the firmware and a few short sound clips.
  • Wi-Fi and Bluetooth LE let it talk to your home network and phone.
  • The cube is 24 by 24 by 16.8 mm and weighs 9.3 grams, about two grapes.

Things you could build

14 ideas sized to what this board can do. Starter ideas suit first-time builders.

Starter ideas

  • Home Assistant voice satellite

    Flash the ready ESPHome config and say the wake word. Turn lights on and off with your voice.

    Uses: Wi-Fi, Hears sound, Makes sound.

  • USB desk speaker and mic

    Plug it into a computer with the factory firmware. It works as a tiny speaker and microphone for calls.

    Uses: Hears sound, Makes sound.

  • Chatty AI helper

    Install the XiaoZhi firmware and ask it questions out loud. It answers through the speaker.

    Uses: Wi-Fi, Hears sound, Makes sound.

  • Kitchen timer you talk to

    Ask for a ten-minute timer while cooking. The speaker chimes when time is up.

    Uses: Wi-Fi, Hears sound, Makes sound.

  • Medicine reminder

    Set daily times in Home Assistant. The cube speaks a gentle reminder to take pills.

    Uses: Wi-Fi, Makes sound.

Builder ideas

  • TV remote by voice

    Use the IR transmitter to send remote control codes. Say a command to change the channel or volume.

    Uses: Wi-Fi, Hears sound.

  • Doorbell chime

    Play a custom sound across the house each time someone presses a wireless doorbell. Pick a new tune each season.

    Uses: Wi-Fi, Makes sound.

  • Plant watering reminder

    Plug a soil moisture sensor into the expansion port. The cube speaks up when the plant is thirsty.

    Uses: Plug-in connectors, Makes sound, Wi-Fi. Add: soil moisture sensor.

  • Classroom quiz buzzer

    Tap the button to answer and the speaker plays a sound. Several cubes make a game show.

    Uses: Makes sound, Bluetooth.

  • Noise level alert

    Listen to a room and flash a warning on Home Assistant once it gets too loud. Teachers and parents get a quiet signal.

    Uses: Hears sound, Wi-Fi.

  • Bedtime story box

    Stream short audio stories over Wi-Fi to the speaker. Kids press the button for the next story.

    Uses: Wi-Fi, Makes sound.

  • Bird song recorder

    Record short clips of the dawn chorus by an open window. Upload them to identify the birds later.

    Uses: Hears sound, Wi-Fi.

Expert ideas

  • Wake word trainer

    Build a custom wake word that runs on the chip with its 8 MB of PSRAM. Give your assistant a new name.

    Uses: Hears sound.

  • Bluetooth phone notifier

    Pair with a phone over Bluetooth LE. The cube reads out alerts from your phone.

    Uses: Bluetooth, Makes sound.

M5Stack posts schematic PDFs for the main board and the audio base, but they carry no license. No PCB layout files were found. The M5Unified library and ESPHome configs are under MIT, ESP-IDF is under Apache-2.0, and the ESP32-S3 chip and its radio code stay closed.
