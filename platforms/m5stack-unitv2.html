Overview

The UnitV2 is a small AI camera from M5Stack with its own Linux computer inside. It runs a 1.2 GHz dual-core chip with 128 MB of memory, a 1080p camera, a microphone, and Wi-Fi. Built-in apps detect faces, colors, shapes, and barcodes from a browser page. It suits makers who want a ready-made vision sensor for robots and Arduino projects.

Good fit or not?

Good for

  • Adding face, color, or barcode detection to a robot
  • Trying computer vision in a browser with no code
  • Learning OpenCV with Python in Jupyter Notebook
  • Sending detection results to an Arduino over one cable

Not great for

  • Fast AI models, as the chip has no AI accelerator
  • Quiet rooms, as a small fan runs to keep it cool
  • Firefox users, as the web page works best in Chrome or Edge
  • Long battery life, with a steady need for USB power

Specs in plain words

  • The 1.2 GHz dual-core processor handles one vision app at a time.
  • 128 MB of memory is room for Linux plus a small vision app.
  • 512 MB of built-in storage holds the system, and the included 32 GB card adds room for photos.
  • The 1080p camera shoots full HD video with a 68 degree view.
  • The UART port sends results as short text messages called JSON.
  • The 48 by 24 mm case weighs about 18 grams, close to four quarters.

Values come from the official docs page and CNX Software. flash_kb is the 512 MB on-chip NAND. CNX says about 100 MB is free. The SSD202D has no NPU, per CNX Software. AI runs on the CPU, likely with NEON. As a result, ai_tops is null. usb_device means the USB-C port acts as a USB Ethernet adapter through an SR9900 chip. The UART is a HY2.0 port. The unit also has a fan, red and white indicator LEDs and one button. Weight is 18 g. The UnitV2-M12 uses a GC2053 sensor with 85 and 150 degree lenses. The UnitV2-USB has no camera and takes a UVC camera on USB-A.

Things you could build

12 ideas sized to what this board can do. Starter ideas suit first-time builders.

Starter ideas

  • Browser face finder

    Open the built-in web page and pick face detection. Boxes appear around faces on the live video.

    Uses: Camera, Runs Linux.

  • Shape sorter demo

    Show circles, squares, and triangles to the camera. Students watch the shape detector name each one.

    Uses: Camera, Runs Linux.

Builder ideas

  • Color tracking robot

    Have the camera follow a colored ball. Send its position over the UART port to a robot that chases it.

    Uses: Camera, Runs Linux. Add: small robot car kit, Grove cable.

  • Barcode pantry log

    Scan barcodes on food packages with the built-in reader. Keep a list of what is in the pantry.

    Uses: Camera, Runs Linux.

  • Family face greeter

    Teach the face recognizer a few family members. A small screen board says hello to each person by name.

    Uses: Camera, Runs Linux. Add: small screen board, Grove cable.

  • Smart vision sensor

    Plug the UART port into an Arduino. The Arduino reads detection results as simple text messages.

    Uses: Camera. Add: Arduino board, Grove cable.

  • Wi-Fi bird watcher

    Stream the camera over Wi-Fi from a window ledge. Run object tracking to spot birds at a feeder.

    Uses: Camera, Wi-Fi, Runs Linux. Add: USB power supply.

  • Jupyter vision lessons

    Open Jupyter Notebook on the camera and write Python with OpenCV. Try edge finding and color filters step by step.

    Uses: Camera, Runs Linux.

Expert ideas

  • Room noise logger

    Record sound levels with the microphone. Log how loud a classroom gets through the day.

    Uses: Hears sound, Runs Linux.

  • Custom object detector

    Train your own NCNN model on a computer and load it onto the camera. It can spot toys, tools, or fruit.

    Uses: Camera, Runs Linux.

  • Parking spot watcher

    Point the camera at a driveway and track cars. Send a message to a phone over Wi-Fi when a spot opens up.

    Uses: Camera, Wi-Fi, Runs Linux. Add: USB power supply, weatherproof box.

  • Hand count game

    Use object tracking to count raised hands. Run a quick classroom poll with the camera as the judge.

    Uses: Camera, Runs Linux.
