Overview

The NASA Artemis Watch 2.0 is a coding smartwatch from CircuitMess. It comes fully assembled with a small color screen, a motion sensor, a clock chip, LEDs, and a buzzer. Kids and classrooms code watch faces, games, and apps in the CircuitBlocks block editor. It launched in April 2026, and the official store now lists it as sold out.

Specs in plain words

  • The ESP32-S3 chip has two fast cores for games and graphics.
  • 4 MB of storage holds many small apps and watch faces.
  • 2 MB of extra working memory helps draw smooth pictures on the screen.
  • The 1.44 inch screen is about the size of a postage stamp.
  • The 600 mAh battery charges over USB-C.
  • Wi-Fi and Bluetooth let it talk to phones and the internet.

Memory values come from the ESP32-S3-MINI-1-N4R2 part shown on the schematic. The schematic labels the screen as a 1.44 inch TFT. By contrast, CNX quoted both 1.14 inch monochrome and 1.44 inch. The FAQ claims a compass and temperature sensor, but the schematic shows only the LSM6DS3TR-C IMU and no magnetometer. CNX reports roughly 2 to 3 hours of battery life per charge. Dimensions were listed as TBD. The buzzer is a simple piezo part. It is not a speaker.
