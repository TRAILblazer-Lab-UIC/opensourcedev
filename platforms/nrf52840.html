Overview

The nRF52840 is a small radio chip from Nordic Semiconductor. It talks Bluetooth Low Energy, Bluetooth mesh, Thread, and Zigbee from one radio. It sleeps on almost no power, which makes it a favorite for wearables and battery sensors. Many maker boards use it, from the thumb-sized Seeed XIAO to the Adafruit Feather and the Arduino Nano 33 BLE Sense.

Good fit or not?

Good for

  • Battery gadgets that need to run for months
  • Bluetooth links to phones, keyboards, and fitness gear
  • Smart home sensors on Thread, Matter, or Zigbee
  • Small tinyML demos with a microphone or motion sensor
  • Learning CircuitPython or Arduino on a wireless board

Not great for

  • Projects that need Wi-Fi to reach the internet
  • Cameras, video, or big AI models
  • Absolute beginners who land in Nordic's own SDKs first
  • Fast audio or graphics work

Specs in plain words

  • The 64 MHz processor is quick for sensors, buttons and radio messages.
  • 256 KB of memory holds a small program and a few thousand sensor readings at once.
  • 1 MB of flash storage fits your program plus a small tinyML model.
  • The deep sleep draw of 0.4 microamps lets a coin cell last a very long time.
  • One radio covers Bluetooth, Thread, and Zigbee, but it has no Wi-Fi.
  • The Adafruit Feather version is about 5 by 2 centimeters, roughly the size of a stick of gum.

Chip values come from the Nordic product page and product specification. The 48 GPIO and 8 ADC channels are for the aQFN73 package. Boards expose fewer, for example 21 GPIO and 6 ADC pins on the Adafruit Feather. Board values are for the Adafruit Feather nRF52840 Express, which adds 2 MB QSPI flash, a NeoPixel, buttons and LiPo charging. The Seeed XIAO nRF52840 is 21 x 17.8 mm with 2 MB QSPI flash. low_power_ua is System OFF at 3 V with no RAM retention.

How open is it?

Openness level

Open CodeOpen BoardWild Open

Level 2 of 3. Wild Open shares the most.

  • Board plansFull design files
  • Hardware licenseOpen license
  • Coding toolsMostly open
  • The chip itselfClosed chip design
  • OSHWACertified open hardware

Several nRF52840 boards are certified open hardware. The Seeed XIAO uses CERN-OHL-W-2.0, and SparkFun shares its files under CC-BY-SA-4.0. Zephyr is Apache-2.0, but Nordic's own SDK code uses a license that limits it to Nordic chips. The Bluetooth stack ships as a closed binary, and the chip itself is proprietary.

Hardware license: CERN-OHL-W-2.0 (Seeed XIAO nRF52840, OSHWA), CC-BY-SA-4.0 (SparkFun Pro nRF52840 Mini), CC-BY-SA (Adafruit) · Software license: LicenseRef-Nordic-5-Clause (nRF Connect SDK), Apache-2.0 (Zephyr)
