nRF54L15

Nordic Semiconductor

A low-power Bluetooth and Thread chip from Nordic for sensors and smart home gadgets.

Field marks: The nRF54L15 pairs an Arm core with a RISC-V helper, a radio for six protocols, and an NFC tag, usually on a XIAO board the size of a thumb.

Overview

The nRF54L15 is a wireless chip from Nordic Semiconductor. It speaks Bluetooth, Thread, Zigbee, and Matter from one radio. It suits makers building battery sensors, wearables, and smart home gadgets. Its standout trait is very low sleep power paired with a fast main core.

Good fit or not?

Good for

  • Battery sensors that report to a phone over Bluetooth
  • Matter and Thread smart home devices
  • Wearables and trackers on a tiny board
  • Projects that need many nodes in a mesh
  • Cheap experiments on the $9.90 Seeed XIAO board

Not great for

  • Wi-Fi projects, as the chip has no Wi-Fi radio
  • Total beginners, as the main tools use Zephyr setup files that feel steep
  • Plug-and-play Arduino use, as its Arduino core is young and community made
  • Direct USB tricks, as the chip itself has no USB

Specs in plain words

  • The 128 MHz main core is about twice as quick as Nordic's older nRF52 chips.
  • 1.5 MB of storage holds a large program plus room for wireless updates.
  • 256 KB of working memory is plenty for sensor data and a wireless stack.
  • Sleep current under one microamp means a coin cell or small battery can last a very long time.
  • A second RISC-V core can act as extra custom hardware pins.
  • The XIAO board is about 21 by 18 millimeters, close to the size of a thumbnail.

Chip values come from the Nordic product page and the November 2024 launch release. The 1.5 MB NVM is on-chip and is shown as flash_kb. Nordic calls it NVM. cores counts the Cortex-M33 and the RISC-V coprocessor. The coprocessor runs software peripherals. The 35 GPIO are for the QFN52 package. QFN48 has 31, CSP47 has 32 and QFN40 has 24. The chip has no USB. Boards use a separate interface chip for USB. Board values are for the Seeed XIAO nRF54L15, which exposes 16 GPIO and adds a battery charger. The XIAO nRF54L15 Sense adds a 6-axis IMU and a PDM microphone. The nRF54L15 DK is 117 x 64 mm with 8 MB of external flash and a J-Link debugger. low_power_ua is the lowest sleep figure on the product page at 3 V.

Starter ideas

  • Bluetooth plant buddy

    Uses: Bluetooth, Sips power. Add: soil moisture sensor.

  • Room temperature beacon

    Uses: Bluetooth, Sips power. Add: temperature sensor.

  • Fridge door alarm

    Sense an open door with a magnet switch and beep after one minute. The board sleeps the rest of the time.

    Uses: Sips power. Add: magnetic door switch, buzzer.

  • Bluetooth button for slides

    Turn a big push button into a wireless clicker. Move to the next slide in class with one tap.

    Uses: Bluetooth. Add: large push button.

Builder ideas

    Count steps with a motion sensor on your wrist. Send the daily total to your phone over Bluetooth.

    Uses: Bluetooth, Battery ready, Sips power. Add: motion sensor, small LiPo battery, wrist strap.

  • Lost keys tag

    Clip a tiny board to your keys and have it chirp a buzzer from a phone command. Find the keys under the couch cushions.

    Uses: Bluetooth, Battery ready, Sips power. Add: buzzer, small LiPo battery.

  • Classroom noise meter

    Listen with a digital microphone and light a green, yellow or red LED. The room gets a gentle hint to quiet down.

    Uses: Hears sound. Add: digital microphone, three LEDs.

  • Pocket tone player

    Play simple tunes through a small speaker over the I2S sound output. Add buttons for a tiny music box.

    Uses: Makes sound. Add: I2S amplifier board, small speaker, push buttons.

  • Bird box visit logger

    Count each visit to a nest box with a motion sensor. Send the tally over Bluetooth on your next walk past.

    Uses: Bluetooth, Sips power, Battery ready. Add: motion sensor, LiPo battery, weatherproof box.

  • Bluetooth game controller

    Wire a joystick and a few buttons. Send moves to a phone or computer game over Bluetooth.

    Uses: Bluetooth. Add: joystick module, push buttons.

  • Heart rate band

    Read a pulse sensor during a walk. Stream the beats per minute to a phone app over Bluetooth.

    Uses: Bluetooth, Battery ready. Add: pulse sensor, small LiPo battery.

Expert ideas

  • Matter smart light switch

    Build a wall switch that joins a Matter smart home over Thread. Control a lamp from your phone or the switch itself.

    Uses: Thread or Zigbee, Mesh messaging. Add: relay module, push button.

    Place several sensor nodes across a garden. Each node passes readings along the Thread mesh back to the house.

    Uses: Thread or Zigbee, Mesh messaging, Sips power, Battery ready. Add: soil moisture sensors, LiPo batteries, weatherproof boxes.

  • Zigbee mailbox alert

    Fit a tilt switch on the mailbox lid. A Zigbee hub tells you the mail has arrived.

    Uses: Thread or Zigbee, Sips power, Battery ready. Add: tilt switch, LiPo battery.

  • Gesture wand

    Read a motion sensor and spot flicks and circles with a small trained model. Each gesture changes the color of an LED strip.

    Add: motion sensor, LED strip.

  • Air quality node for the neighborhood

    Uses: Bluetooth, Sips power. Add: air quality sensor, weatherproof box.

How open is it?

Openness level

Open CodeOpen BoardWild Open

Level 2 of 3. Wild Open shares the most.

  • Board plansFull design files
  • Hardware licenseOpen license
  • Coding toolsMostly open
  • The chip itselfClosed chip design
  • OSHWACertified open hardware

The Seeed XIAO nRF54L15 is certified open hardware under CERN-OHL-W-2.0, with schematics and a KiCad project. The nRF Connect SDK builds on Zephyr under Apache-2.0, but Nordic's own code uses a license tied to Nordic chips. The Bluetooth controller ships as a closed library, and the chip itself is a closed design.

Hardware license: CERN-OHL-W-2.0 (Seeed XIAO nRF54L15, OSHWA CN000027), CERN-OHL-P-2.0 (Icy Electronics nRF54L15 Discovery repo) · Software license: LicenseRef-Nordic-5-Clause (nRF Connect SDK), Apache-2.0 (Zephyr)
