Overview

The nRF54LM20A is a wireless chip from Nordic Semiconductor. It handles Bluetooth, Thread, Zigbee, Matter, and NFC on one radio. It has more memory than most of its relatives and sips very little power. Most makers meet it on the thumb-sized Seeed XIAO nRF54LM20A board.

Good fit or not?

Good for

  • Battery sensors that run for months
  • Smart home gadgets for Matter, Thread, or Zigbee
  • Bluetooth controllers, trackers, and wearables
  • Sound and motion projects with the Sense board

Not great for

  • Wi-Fi projects, as it has no Wi-Fi radio
  • First-time coders, as most examples are C programs
  • Arduino fans who want polished support, as the Arduino core is new and community made
  • Long-range links across a town

Specs in plain words

  • A 128 MHz main processor plus a helper core is quick enough for sensor math and simple sound work.
  • 512 KB of working memory holds many thousands of sensor readings at once.
  • 2 MB of built-in storage leaves plenty of room for wireless code and your own program.
  • A sleep draw under one microamp lets a coin cell last a very long time.
  • The XIAO board is about 21 by 18 millimeters, smaller than a postage stamp.
  • The XIAO has a battery charger built in for a small LiPo cell.

Chip values come from the Nordic product page and the September 2025 announcement. The 2 MB NVM is on-chip and is shown as flash_kb. cores counts the Cortex-M33 and the RISC-V coprocessor. The 66 GPIO are for the CSP98 package. CSP61 has 40 and QFN52 has 32. CSP98 and CSP61 are in mass production. QFN52 is in early sampling. Transmit power is +8 dBm on CSP and +7 dBm on QFN. The interface list maps TDM to i2s. The product page does not say whether USB can act as host. Board values are for the Seeed XIAO nRF54LM20A. It has 28 GPIO, 9 analog pins, 8 MB external flash, an nPM1300 PMIC with LiPo charging, an RGB LED and an IPEX4 antenna connector. The XIAO Sense adds a 6-axis IMU and a PDM microphone. The XIAO draws about 4.76 uA in System OFF and 0.33 uA in ship mode.

Builder ideas

  • Bluetooth plant moisture tag

    Stick a soil sensor in a pot and read the moisture on your phone. A small battery lasts for months.

    Uses: Bluetooth, Battery ready, Sips power. Add: soil moisture sensor, small LiPo battery.

  • Door and window sensor

    Put a magnet switch on a door and send a note over Zigbee each time it opens. Your home hub logs every visit.

    Uses: Thread or Zigbee, Sips power, Battery ready. Add: magnetic reed switch, coin cell holder.

  • Step counter for class

    Use the Sense board's motion sensor to count steps. Students compare their totals on a phone at the end of the day.

    Uses: Bluetooth, Battery ready. Add: small LiPo battery.

  • Clap-activated lamp

    Listen through the Sense board's microphone for two claps. Then switch a lamp on with a relay.

    Uses: Hears sound. Add: relay module, lamp.

  • Bluetooth game controller

    Wire up a few buttons and a joystick. The board shows up on a computer as a wireless gamepad.

    Uses: Bluetooth, Battery ready. Add: joystick module, push buttons, small LiPo battery.

  • Lost keys finder

    Hang a tiny board on your keyring. A phone app shows how close it is and the board beeps on request.

    Uses: Bluetooth, Sips power, Makes sound. Add: small buzzer, coin cell holder.

  • Desk posture reminder

    Clip the Sense board to your shirt. It buzzes after you slouch for too long.

    Uses: Battery ready, Makes sound. Add: small vibration motor, small LiPo battery.

Expert ideas

  • Matter smart light switch

    Build a wall button that turns smart lights on and off over Thread. It joins a Matter home hub.

    Uses: Thread or Zigbee, Mesh messaging, Battery ready. Add: push button, small LiPo battery.

  • Garden mesh weather network

    Spread several boards across a garden and pass readings along a Thread mesh. One board collects them all.

    Uses: Thread or Zigbee, Mesh messaging, Sips power. Add: temperature and humidity sensor, small LiPo battery.

  • Gesture remote

    Flick your wrist to skip a song or pause a video. The motion sensor picks up the gesture and Bluetooth sends the command.

    Uses: Bluetooth, Battery ready. Add: wristband, small LiPo battery.

  • Beehive weight scale

    Weigh a beehive each hour to track honey and swarms. The reading goes to your phone over Bluetooth.

    Uses: Bluetooth, Sips power, Battery ready. Add: load cell, load cell amplifier, small LiPo battery.

  • Bird box noise logger

    Record how loud a bird box gets through the day with the microphone. Download the daily chart over Bluetooth.

    Uses: Hears sound, Bluetooth, Sips power. Add: small LiPo battery, weatherproof box.

The Seeed XIAO nRF54LM20A has published KiCad files and is certified open hardware under CERN-OHL-W-2.0. The Zephyr base of the software is Apache-2.0, and Nordic's own SDK code uses the Nordic 5-Clause license. The chip itself and the Bluetooth controller library stay closed.
