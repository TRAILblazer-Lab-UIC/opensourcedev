Overview

The Jetson AGX Orin Developer Kit is NVIDIA's most powerful computer of the Orin family. It packs a 2048-core graphics chip and 64 GB of memory into a cube about 11 centimeters wide. It runs JetPack, which is Ubuntu Linux plus NVIDIA's AI tools. It suits robot builders, researchers, and labs that need large AI models on a desk.

Good fit or not?

Good for

  • Running larger language and vision models with no cloud
  • Robots that watch through several cameras at once
  • Building apps for smaller Jetson boards before buying them
  • Fast networks and fast SSD storage
  • Learning the AI tools used in industry

Not great for

  • Beginners and tight budgets
  • Quiet rooms
  • Battery gadgets or tiny sensors
  • HDMI screens without an adapter

Specs in plain words

  • 275 TOPS means it does 275 trillion small math steps each second for AI.
  • 64 GB of memory is enough to hold a fairly large chat model in one go.
  • The 12-core 2.2 GHz processor runs Linux as fast as a good desktop computer.
  • It draws 15 to 60 watts, about as much as a bright light bulb.
  • The 10 gigabit network port moves data about ten times faster than a normal home port.
  • The cube is 11 centimeters wide and about 7 centimeters tall, roughly a coffee mug in size.

Size110 x 110 mm

Height is 71.65 mm. Flash is the 64 GB eMMC 5.1 on the module. An NVMe SSD can be added in the M.2 Key M slot. The only display output is DisplayPort 1.4a. HDMI needs an adapter. The M.2 Key E slot ships with a wireless card, described by a review as an AzureWave AW-CB375NF with 802.11ac and Bluetooth 5.0 (search snippet). The 90 W supply figure comes from SparkFun and review snippets. NVIDIA's layout page names the DC jack size but not its voltage. An earlier 32 GB kit variant existed. This card uses the current 64 GB kit.

Builder ideas

  • Private chat assistant

    Run a mid-sized language model on the kit's 64 GB of memory. Ask questions with no internet connection at all.

    Uses: AI chip, Runs Linux. Add: NVMe SSD.

  • Object spotting webcam

    Plug in a USB webcam and run a ready-made detection demo. The screen draws boxes around people, cups, and pets.

    Uses: Camera, Plugs in USB gadgets, AI chip, Runs Linux. Add: USB webcam, DisplayPort screen.

  • Photo album search

    Index thousands of family photos with a vision model. Type a phrase like grandma at the beach and see matches.

    Uses: AI chip, Runs Linux. Add: NVMe SSD.

  • Ask questions about pictures

    Run a vision and language model together. Show it a photo of a plant and ask what it needs.

    Uses: AI chip, Runs Linux. Add: USB webcam.

  • Wildlife camera that names animals

    Point a camera at a feeder or trail. The kit saves clips and labels each visitor by species.

    Uses: Camera, AI chip, Runs Linux. Add: camera module, weatherproof box, NVMe SSD.

  • Live art style mirror

    Run a style transfer model on webcam video. Visitors see themselves as a moving painting on a big screen.

    Uses: Camera, Plugs in USB gadgets, AI chip, Runs Linux. Add: USB webcam, DisplayPort screen.

  • Bluetooth game controller robot

    Pair a wireless game controller over Bluetooth. Drive a camera robot around the house and watch its AI view.

    Uses: Bluetooth, Camera, AI chip, Runs Linux. Add: wireless game controller, robot rover chassis, motor driver board, camera module.

  • Community meeting captioner

    Run a speech to text model on a room microphone. Live captions appear on a screen for neighbors who are hard of hearing.

    Uses: Hears sound, Plugs in USB gadgets, AI chip, Runs Linux. Add: USB microphone, DisplayPort screen.

  • Science lab microscope counter

    Connect a USB microscope. A model counts cells, seeds, or pollen grains in each image.

    Uses: Camera, Plugs in USB gadgets, AI chip, Runs Linux. Add: USB microscope.

Expert ideas

  • Talking voice helper

    Pair a speech model with a language model. Ask questions out loud and hear spoken answers.

    Uses: Hears sound, Makes sound, Plugs in USB gadgets, AI chip, Runs Linux. Add: USB microphone, small speaker.

  • Multi-camera street watcher

    Connect several camera modules to the wide camera connector. The kit counts bikes, cars, and walkers on each view at once.

    Uses: Camera, AI chip, Runs Linux. Add: several camera modules, camera adapter board.

  • Self-driving robot rover

    Mount the cube on a rover with cameras up front. The AI steers around chairs and follows hallways.

    Uses: Camera, AI chip, Runs Linux. Add: robot rover chassis, motor driver board, camera module, large battery pack.

  • Classroom AI server

    Set the kit up on the school network. Students send prompts from their laptops to one shared local model.

    Uses: Wired network, AI chip, Runs Linux. Add: network cable.

  • Song and sound tagger

    Feed recordings into an audio model. It labels bird calls, instruments, or crowd noise in each clip.

    Uses: Hears sound, AI chip, Runs Linux. Add: USB microphone.

  • Robot arm that sorts objects

    Use the 40-pin header and a camera to guide a small robot arm. The arm picks up blocks by color or shape.

    Uses: Camera, AI chip, Runs Linux. Add: small robot arm, servo driver board, camera module.

  • Fall alert for a loved one

    Watch a room with a pose model. The kit sends a message to family over Wi-Fi after a fall.

    Uses: Camera, Wi-Fi, AI chip, Runs Linux. Add: camera module.

  • Test bench for smaller Jetsons

    Reflash the kit to act like an Orin Nano or Orin NX. Build your app here before you buy the smaller board.

    Uses: Runs Linux.

Open Board

NVIDIA publishes the carrier board schematics, layout, Gerbers, and parts list after a free developer login. The license on those files was not confirmed. The Linux kernel is GPL-2.0 and NVIDIA shares its board support sources. The AGX Orin module, CUDA, and TensorRT stay closed under NVIDIA license terms.
