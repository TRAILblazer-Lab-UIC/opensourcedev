Overview

The Jetson AGX Thor Developer Kit is NVIDIA's most powerful edge AI computer. It pairs a Blackwell graphics chip with 128 GB of memory and a 1 TB SSD. It runs JetPack 7, which is Ubuntu 24.04 plus NVIDIA's AI tools. It suits robot labs and researchers who build humanoid robots or run very large models locally.

Good fit or not?

Good for

  • Very large language and vision models with no cloud
  • Humanoid and research robots
  • Running several AI jobs at the same time
  • Fast sensor networks for many cameras
  • Learning the AI tools used on Arm servers

Not great for

  • Beginners and almost every budget
  • Hobby add-ons made for a Raspberry Pi style pin header
  • Simple ribbon cable camera modules
  • Battery gadgets or small portable builds

Specs in plain words

  • 2070 TFLOPS means it can do about two thousand trillion tiny AI math steps each second.
  • 128 GB of memory can hold a very large chat model in one go.
  • The 14-core processor is the kind of chip found in modern Arm servers.
  • It draws 40 to 130 watts, about as much as a gaming laptop.
  • The 1 TB SSD holds hundreds of AI models or many hours of video.
  • The box is about 24 by 11 centimeters, close to a hardcover book laid flat.

Height is 56.88 mm with feet and thermal solution. The ai_tops value is NVIDIA's 2070 FP4 sparse TFLOPS figure. The 2.6 GHz CPU clock appears in NVIDIA's spec table without a per-product label. Storage is the included 1 TB NVMe SSD in the M.2 Key M slot. Onboard module eMMC was not confirmed. As a result, flash_kb is null. There is no MIPI CSI connector on the kit. Cameras connect over the QSFP Holoscan Sensor Bridge or USB. NVIDIA's spec table lists no 40-pin header, though one NVIDIA promo page mentions one.

How open is it?

Open Board

NVIDIA publishes the carrier board schematics, layout, and Gerbers, likely behind a developer login. The license on those files was not confirmed. The Linux kernel is GPL-2.0 and NVIDIA shares its board support sources. The T5000 module, CUDA, and TensorRT stay closed under NVIDIA license terms.
