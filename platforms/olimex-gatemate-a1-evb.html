Overview

The GateMateA1-EVB is an open-source hardware board from Olimex. It carries a GateMate A1 FPGA from Cologne Chip in Germany. An FPGA is a chip you rewire with code to become almost any digital circuit. The board has VGA and PS/2 ports and suits people who want to build retro computers with fully open tools.

Good fit or not?

Good for

  • Learning Verilog with the open Yosys and nextpnr tools
  • Retro computers and arcade games on a VGA screen
  • Soft processors that need extra memory
  • Students who want a low-cost European FPGA

Not great for

  • Complete beginners, as it needs a hardware language
  • Wireless projects, as it has no radio
  • Analog sensors, as it has no analog inputs
  • Quick tutorials, as guides are few and some use older tools

Specs in plain words

  • 20,480 programmable elements is room for an old 8-bit computer and its video circuit together.
  • 1,280 Kbit of block RAM is fast memory inside the chip for screens and buffers.
  • 8 MB of PSRAM gives a soft processor room for bigger programs.
  • The onboard RP2040 acts as the programmer. One USB-C cable handles power and uploads.
  • VGA and PS/2 ports connect an old monitor and keyboard.
  • Pmod and UEXT ports take small plug-in add-ons.

ram_kb is the FPGA's 1,280 Kbit of block RAM. psram_kb is the 64 Mbit PSRAM chip. flash_kb is the 2 MB flash listed as configuration flash for the RP2040. The -2M variant adds a second 2 MB SPI flash (U3) next to the FPGA. The FPGA has 162 GPIOs in its BGA package. The number routed to headers was not found. As a result, gpio is null. Interfaces listed are those exposed on the UEXT and Pmod connectors plus JTAG through the RP2040. Other interfaces are soft logic. The board also has VGA and PS/2 connectors, which have no tag in the allowed list. Core voltage is adjustable from 0.9 V to 1.1 V per a user manual summary (search snippet).

Things you could build

12 ideas sized to what this board can do. Starter ideas suit first-time builders.

Builder ideas

  • Blink and button lab

    Write a small Verilog design that lights the LED from the buttons. It shows how logic circuits react at once.

  • VGA color bars

    Draw color bars and moving boxes on an old monitor. It teaches how a screen is painted line by line.

    Add: VGA monitor, VGA cable.

  • Pong for two players

    Build Pong fully in logic and play with the board buttons. No processor runs the game.

    Add: VGA monitor.

  • Sensor reader on UEXT

    Plug a sensor into the UEXT port and show its reading on the screen. It is a good bridge from logic to real data.

    Add: UEXT temperature sensor module.

  • Chiptune music box

    Make square wave tunes in logic and play them through a speaker. Each button picks a song.

    Add: small speaker, small amplifier board.

  • Digital art screensaver

    Draw bouncing patterns and color waves on a VGA screen. Hang the monitor in a hallway as moving art.

    Add: VGA monitor.

  • Servo controller lab

    Generate precise pulses through a Pmod port to swing a servo. Use it to point a pointer or wave a flag.

    Add: servo motor, jumper wires.

Expert ideas

  • Space Invaders arcade

    Load an 8080 processor design in Verilog and run the classic Space Invaders game. Play it on a VGA screen.

    Add: VGA monitor, PS/2 keyboard.

  • Retro PC emulator

    Build an old IBM PC style computer in logic. Type on a PS/2 keyboard and watch text on a VGA monitor.

    Add: VGA monitor, PS/2 keyboard.

  • Typing tutor game

    Show letters on the screen and score how fast a player types them. Students can race each other.

    Add: VGA monitor, PS/2 keyboard.

  • Soft RISC-V computer

    Load a small RISC-V processor into the FPGA and run C programs on it. The PSRAM gives it room to grow.

  • Homemade logic analyzer

    Capture fast signals from another circuit into memory. View them later as waveforms on your computer.

    Add: jumper wires.

Olimex publishes KiCad files and Gerbers for every board revision under CERN-OHL-S-2.0. The board is OSHWA certified as BG000117. The design tools Yosys, nextpnr, and openFPGALoader are open. The GateMate chip itself is closed.
