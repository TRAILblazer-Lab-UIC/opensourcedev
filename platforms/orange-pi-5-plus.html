Orange Pi 5 Plus

Shenzhen Xunlong Software (Orange Pi)

A powerful eight-core Linux board with two fast network ports and an AI helper.

Field marks: Two 2.5 Gigabit ports, an NVMe slot, and an HDMI input crowd the Orange Pi 5 Plus, a board with enough jacks to replace your router, NAS, and media player.

Overview

Orange Pi 5 Plus is a Linux computer about the size of a palm. It uses the eight-core Rockchip RK3588 with a 6 TOPS AI helper. Two 2.5 gigabit network ports, a fast NVMe slot, and an HDMI input make it stand out. It suits tinkerers who want a small home server, media box or AI test bench.

Good fit or not?

Good for

  • Home servers, file storage, and routers
  • Big screens up to 8K over HDMI
  • Running small AI vision models on camera feeds
  • A quiet desktop computer with many screens
  • Capturing video through the HDMI input

Not great for

  • Absolute beginners who want plug-and-play ease
  • Wireless projects without an extra M.2 Wi-Fi card
  • Tight budgets. Prices have risen to about 367 US dollars for the 16 GB board.
  • Fully open hardware fans, as only a schematic is public

Specs in plain words

  • Eight processor cores at up to 2.4 GHz feel close to a modest laptop.
  • 4, 8, or 16 GB of memory runs a desktop, a browser, and several servers at once.
  • 6 TOPS means the AI helper does about 6 trillion small math steps each second.
  • Two 2.5 gigabit network ports move files more than twice as fast as common home ports.
  • The NVMe slot takes a fast SSD drive for storage and booting.
  • The board is about 10 by 7.5 centimeters, close to a large playing card.

RAM comes in 4, 8 and 16 GB versions. Ram_kb shows the 8 GB board. There is no soldered eMMC. Flash_kb is null because storage is a plug-in eMMC module (16 to 256 GB), microSD or NVMe. The board also has 16 MB or 32 MB of QSPI NOR flash for the bootloader. Wi-Fi and Bluetooth need an optional M.2 E-key module. As a result, only Ethernet is listed as a radio. The 40-pin header GPIO count was not stated on the official page. It has two USB 3.0 and two USB 2.0 Type-A ports and two USB-C ports, one for power. Audio uses an ES8388 codec with a 3.5 mm jack, a MIC input and a speaker connector. The battery connector is a 2-pin RTC backup battery header. The official page lists 100 x 75 mm and 86.5 g. Notebookcheck reported 100 x 70 mm.
