Overview

Orange Pi Zero 3 is a small Linux computer about 5 centimeters on each side. It uses the Allwinner H618 with four Arm cores and a small graphics chip. It has Gigabit Ethernet, Wi-Fi 5, Bluetooth 5.0 and a micro HDMI port. It suits tinkerers who want a quiet network helper, media player or retro game box.

Good fit or not?

Good for

  • Always-on network tools such as ad blockers
  • Small home servers that sit out of sight
  • Retro gaming and media on a TV
  • Learning Linux on a cheap board
  • Tight spaces where a full-size board will not fit

Not great for

  • Absolute beginners who want plug-and-play ease
  • Camera projects. It has no camera connector.
  • Desktop use with a big browser on the 1 GB board
  • Audio projects without the extra adapter board

Specs in plain words

  • Four cores at 1.5 GHz handle servers and light media with ease.
  • 1 to 4 GB of memory fits a few small servers at once.
  • The Gigabit port moves files as fast as most home networks allow.
  • The micro HDMI port shows video up to 4K on a TV.
  • 16 MB of flash holds only a startup program. Your system lives on a microSD card.
  • The board is about 5 by 5.5 centimeters, close to a sticky note.

RAM comes in 1, 1.5, 2 and 4 GB versions. Ram_kb shows the 1 GB entry board. flash_kb is null because there is no eMMC. The board boots from microSD, and the 16 MB SPI flash holds a bootloader. The 26-pin header carries I2C, SPI, UART and GPIO. The GPIO count was not stated on the official page. The 13-pin header carries stereo audio out, a mic input, CVBS TV out, IR receive and two USB 2.0 ports, and needs an adapter board. Power management uses an AXP313A PMIC. The Wi-Fi and Bluetooth module is a CdTech 20U5622 per CNX Software. The official page lists 50 x 55 mm. CNX lists 55 x 50 mm.

Orange Pi publishes a schematic PDF, a mechanical file, and Linux and Android sources. The orangepi-build system uses GPL-2.0. No board layout files or hardware license are published. The H618 chip is closed, and the Wi-Fi and graphics parts need vendor firmware.
