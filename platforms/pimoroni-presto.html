Overview

Presto is a desktop touchscreen from Pimoroni with a Raspberry Pi RP2350 chip inside. It has a 4 inch square color screen in a black metal stand, Wi-Fi, a speaker, a memory card slot, and seven colored lights on the back. It ships with MicroPython and a menu of ready examples. It suits makers, teachers, and families who want a smart screen for the desk.

Good fit or not?

Good for

  • Wi-Fi dashboards for weather, news, or calendars
  • Photo frames and clocks
  • Showing readings from plug-in sensors
  • Small touch games and art apps

Not great for

  • Battery projects, as it has no charger on board
  • Wiring many sensors or motors, as it has only one plug-in sensor port
  • Bluetooth projects, as MicroPython support for it is unconfirmed
  • Tight budgets, as a bare board and screen cost less

Specs in plain words

  • Two 150 MHz cores are quick enough for smooth touch menus and simple animations.
  • 8 MB of extra memory holds full screen pictures and fonts at once.
  • 16 MB of storage holds your programs and the example launcher.
  • The 4 inch screen is about the size of a coaster, with 480 by 480 dots.
  • Wi-Fi lets it fetch the time, weather, and other live data from the internet.
  • The microSD slot adds room for lots of photos and files.
Full spec sheet
ProcessorRaspberry Pi RP2350B Arm Cortex-M33 (or Hazard3 RISC-V), 2 cores at 150 MHz
Memory520 KB plus 8 MB extra (PSRAM)
Storage16 MB
Ways to connect partsI2C, SPI, USB device, SD card bus, Screen bus, Debug port
RadiosWi-Fi, Bluetooth Low Energy
ConnectorsUSB-C, Qwiic or STEMMA QT, microSD slot, Battery plug
Built inscreen, color LED, speaker, buttons
Power5 V over USB-C, or 3 V to 5.5 V on the JST-PH battery connector
Size110 x 92 mm

Flash is a W25Q128JV and PSRAM is an APS6404L per the schematic. The display uses an RGB parallel bus with SPI setup lines, and the touch controller sits on its own I2C pins. The microSD slot is wired with four data lines. As a result, sdio is listed. No general GPIO header is exposed. Expansion is through the Qw/ST I2C port. As a result, gpio and adc_channels are null. The 3-pin JST-SH debug connector appears only on units made after June 2025. Pimoroni says Bluetooth without naming a version. The CYW43439 supports Bluetooth LE. As a result, ble is listed. Size is 110 x 92 x 80 mm (H x W x D) including the stand.

How open is it?

Openness level

Open CodeOpen BoardWild Open

Level 2 of 3. Wild Open shares the most.

  • Board plansSchematics only
  • Hardware licenseNot confirmed
  • Coding toolsMostly open
  • The chip itselfClosed chip design

Pimoroni publishes a schematic PDF with no license and no board layout files. The Presto firmware, examples, and C++ boilerplate are open source under the MIT license. The Wi-Fi chip needs a closed firmware file from Infineon. The RP2350 has open RISC-V cores, but the chip as a whole is closed.
