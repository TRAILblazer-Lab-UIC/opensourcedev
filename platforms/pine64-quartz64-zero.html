Overview

The Quartz64-Zero is a low-cost single-board computer from Pine64. It uses a Rockchip RK3566T chip with 1 GB of memory, Wi-Fi 6, and Bluetooth 5.4. It is about the size of a Raspberry Pi and costs about 20 dollars. Pine64 promises supply until at least 2028, which suits long-lived products.

Good fit or not?

Good for

  • Low-cost media players on Android
  • Product makers who need a board that stays on sale for years
  • Experienced users who want to port Linux to new hardware
  • Adding a Pi-style PCIe accessory on a budget

Not great for

  • Beginners. The only official images are Android 13 builds.
  • Wired network projects. The Ethernet port is an empty footprint on the default board.
  • Camera or audio jack projects. Those parts are unpopulated too.
  • Heavy apps. 1 GB of memory fills up fast.

Specs in plain words

  • The four-core 1.6 GHz processor handles video, simple apps, and light servers.
  • 1 GB of memory is enough for a media player or a small script. It is tight for a desktop.
  • Wi-Fi 6 and Bluetooth 5.4 are built in. No dongle is needed.
  • HDMI output reaches 4K at 60 frames per second for crisp video on a TV.
  • The 40-pin header arrives unsoldered. You solder on the pins yourself.
  • The board is about the size of a deck of cards laid flat.

Storage is a microSD card or an optional eMMC module. As a result, flash_kb is null. CNX Software says Ethernet, the audio jack, two USB 2.0 ports, MIPI DSI, MIPI CSI and SPI flash are footprints only on the default board. As a result, they are not listed. CNX Software was unsure whether the RK3566T has the 0.8 TOPS NPU. As a result, ai_tops is null. The 40-pin header ships unsoldered. The exact GPIO count was not confirmed. CNX Software also lists a mini PCIe socket, an RTC battery connector and an IR receiver. Size 85 x 56 x 18.8 mm is from the Pine64 schematics page.

How open is it?

Openness level

Open CodeOpen BoardWild Open

Level 2 of 3. Wild Open shares the most.

  • Board plansSchematics only
  • Hardware licenseNot confirmed
  • Coding toolsMostly open
  • The chip itselfClosed chip design

Pine64 publishes the schematic and part placement drawings as PDFs. No layout files are offered and no license is stated. The graphics use the open Panfrost driver, and Android parts use Apache-2.0. The Rockchip chip design is closed, and the Wi-Fi chip needs closed firmware.

Software license: various (Linux GPL-2.0, Android Apache-2.0)
