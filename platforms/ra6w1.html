Overview

The RA6W1 is a wireless microcontroller from Renesas Electronics. It pairs a 160 MHz Arm Cortex-M33 with dual-band Wi-Fi 6. Renesas aims it at Matter devices, smart home products, and battery Wi-Fi sensors. It suits engineers and experienced makers who already write C.

Good fit or not?

Good for

  • Wi-Fi sensors that need to sleep and save battery
  • Matter and smart home product experiments
  • Devices that use the quieter 5 GHz Wi-Fi band
  • Products that must stay on sale for many years, with Renesas promising 15 years of supply

Not great for

  • Beginners, with no Arduino, MicroPython, or Zephyr support found
  • Bluetooth projects, which need the sister RA6W2 chip
  • Cheap hobby builds, with the only board costing about 69 dollars
  • Projects that need lots of shared examples, with very few online so far

Specs in plain words

  • A 160 MHz processor is plenty for reading sensors and talking to the internet.
  • 704 KB of working memory holds a Wi-Fi stack plus your own program with room to spare.
  • The chip has no built-in storage. The kit adds 8 MB of flash memory and 8 MB of extra RAM.
  • Wi-Fi 6 with target wake time lets a sensor sleep between check-ins and save battery.
  • Microphone inputs and a sound output link let it record or play simple audio.
  • Up to 28 pins connect buttons, sensors, and small add-on boards.

Chip specs come from CNX Software and the Renesas kit page. The chip has no internal flash. psram_kb and flash_kb are the EK-RA6W1's 8 MB QSPI flash and 8 MB PSRAM. Radios cover the RA6W1 only. The RA6W2 and the RRQ61051 module add Bluetooth LE 5.1. The RA6W1 has a coexistence interface for an external BLE or Zigbee radio. The kit has an onboard power measurement circuit that reads down to 1 uA. The kit connector type, size and buttons were not confirmed. usb_c and buttons are not verified.
