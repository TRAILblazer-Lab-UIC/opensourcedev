Radxa ROCK 5C

Radxa

A credit card sized eight-core Linux computer with an AI helper and Wi-Fi 6.

Field marks: A board that copies the Raspberry Pi 5 layout down to the PCIe connector, then adds eight CPU cores, a 6 TOPS NPU, and up to 32 GB of RAM.

Overview

Radxa ROCK 5C is a Linux computer about the size of a credit card. It uses the eight-core Rockchip RK3588S2 chip with a 6 TOPS AI helper. It copies the Raspberry Pi 5 shape and fits many of the same add-on boards. It suits tinkerers who want a small desktop, media player, home server, or AI test bench.

Good fit or not?

Good for

  • A quiet desktop computer that sits behind a screen
  • Media playback on big screens up to 8K
  • Running small AI vision models on camera feeds
  • Home servers on Wi-Fi 6 or wired Gigabit Ethernet
  • Reusing Raspberry Pi 5 style NVMe add-on boards

Not great for

  • Absolute beginners who want plug-and-play ease
  • Tight budgets. Prices for the bigger memory sizes have jumped well above launch prices.
  • Fans of fully open hardware. Only schematics and drawings are public.
  • Quick projects. Every setup starts with an operating system install.

Specs in plain words

  • Eight processor cores at up to 2.4 GHz feel close to a modest laptop.
  • Memory comes in sizes from 2 GB to 32 GB. The 8 GB board runs a desktop, a browser, and a few servers at once.
  • 6 TOPS means the AI helper does about 6 trillion small math steps each second.
  • The PCIe ribbon connector adds a fast NVMe drive through an adapter board.
  • The board is 86 by 56 millimeters, about the size of a credit card.
  • The ROCK 5C Lite has two big cores and no graphics chip. It costs less but does less.

RAM comes in 2, 4, 8, 16 and 32 GB on the ROCK 5C and 1 to 16 GB on the Lite. Ram_kb shows the 8 GB board. There is no soldered eMMC. Storage is an eMMC module, microSD or NVMe over the PCIe FPC. As a result, flash_kb is null. There is no SPI flash. USB is two USB 2.0 host ports, one USB 3 host and one USB 3 OTG port. The 40-pin header functions come from the product page. The GPIO count was not confirmed. The ROCK 5C Lite (RK3582) has no GPU, a 5 TOPS NPU and 4Kp60 encode in place of 8Kp30.

How open is it?

Openness level

Open CodeOpen BoardWild Open

Level 2 of 3. Wild Open shares the most.

  • Board plansSchematics only
  • Hardware licenseNot confirmed
  • Coding toolsMostly open
  • The chip itselfClosed chip design

Radxa publishes schematic PDFs, placement maps, drawings, and a 3D model for two board revisions. The build scripts use GPL-3.0 and the Linux kernel uses GPL-2.0. No board layout files or hardware license are published. The RK3588S2 chip is closed, and the AI and graphics tools include closed Rockchip files.
