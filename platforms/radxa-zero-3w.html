Overview

Radxa ZERO 3W is a tiny Linux computer the size of a stick of gum. It uses a four-core Rockchip RK3566 chip with a small AI helper. It matches the Raspberry Pi Zero 2 W shape but offers up to 8 GB of memory. It suits tinkerers who want a small media player, camera or network gadget.

How the scores work

Good fit or not?

Good for

  • Hidden media players and photo frames
  • Small Wi-Fi cameras with a Pi camera module
  • Network helpers such as ad blockers
  • Learning Linux on a very small and cheap board
  • Retro gaming on a TV

Not great for

  • Absolute beginners. It needs an operating system install and some adapters.
  • Wired network projects. The 3W has no Ethernet port.
  • Heavy AI work. The AI helper is small.
  • Sound projects. The board has no audio jack.

Specs in plain words

  • Four cores at 1.6 GHz handle a light desktop, a media player or a small server.
  • Memory comes in 1, 2, 4 or 8 GB. The 4 GB board runs a desktop and a browser at once.
  • 0.8 TOPS means the AI helper does almost a trillion small math steps each second. That fits simple vision models.
  • The camera port takes Raspberry Pi Camera V1.3 and V2 modules.
  • The board is 65 by 30 millimeters, about the size of a stick of gum.
  • Both USB ports are USB-C. Most USB gadgets need a small adapter.

RAM comes in 1, 2, 4 and 8 GB. Ram_kb shows the 4 GB board. eMMC is 0, 8, 16, 32 or 64 GB by model. As a result, flash_kb is null. All models have a microSD slot. The 40-pin header is optional. Some SKUs ship without it soldered. Header functions were not read from a fetched page. As a result, I2C, SPI and UART are not listed. The NPU figure comes from CNX Software. The Radxa docs table does not list it. CNX names the wireless part as the Radxa Wireless Module D8. A V1.12 board revision uses an AIC8800 chip.

How open is it?

Open Board

Radxa publishes schematics, placement diagrams, drawings and 3D models for the board. The build scripts use GPL-3.0 and the Linux kernel uses GPL-2.0. No board layout files or hardware license are published. The RK3566 chip is closed, and the Wi-Fi firmware and AI tools include closed files.
