Overview

The RAK WisMesh Tap V2 is a finished handheld radio for Meshtastic text messaging. It has a color touchscreen with an on-screen keyboard. It sends messages with no phone at all. GPS, a buzzer, a big battery, and a weatherproof case are all inside. It suits hikers, emergency teams, and community groups who want a ready device with no soldering.

Good fit or not?

Good for

  • Texting off the grid straight from the screen
  • Offline maps from a microSD card
  • Sharing your location with a group
  • Rainy or dusty outdoor trips in its sealed case
  • Long days away from power on one charge

Not great for

  • Quick pocket carry, as it is a chunky device
  • Adding your own sensors, as the pin headers sit inside the sealed case
  • Fast charging from a laptop port, as RAK suggests a 5 volt 2 amp wall adapter
  • People who want open hardware files, as RAK shares only schematic pictures

Specs in plain words

  • The ESP32-S3 chip runs up to 240 MHz. It has plenty of speed for maps and a touchscreen.
  • 8 MB of extra memory holds the screen pictures and map tiles as you scroll.
  • The 320 by 240 screen is about the size of a playing card cut in half.
  • The LoRa radio comes in an 800 MHz or 900 MHz edition. Buy the band your country allows for licence-free use.
  • The 3200 mAh battery is about the size of a phone battery. It powers the device through a long day outdoors.
  • The IP65 rating means the case keeps out dust and splashing rain.

Core values come from the RAK3312 datasheet, which lists 16 MB flash, 8 MB PSRAM, 512 KB SRAM and deep sleep down to 8 uA for the module alone. Device sleep current is unknown. The datasheet does not state a clock. 240 MHz is the ESP32-S3 maximum. The quick start says all GPIOs are brought out on pin headers inside the case. The battery and solar connectors are JST plugs on the RAK19007 base board. Store page and Meshtastic docs list a 3200 mAh battery. The RAK docs overview says 3600 mAh. The store lists a 2.3 dBi external blade antenna. The RAK overview says 2 dBi. Antenna connector is SMA per Meshtastic docs. The buzzer is not in the onboard vocab. Case size and weight were not published on pages read.

Starter ideas

  • Phone-free trail messenger

    Type messages on the touchscreen keyboard and send them over the mesh. Your hiking group stays in touch with no cell signal.

    Uses: LoRa long range radio, Mesh messaging, Screen.

  • Offline map on the trail

    Load map tiles onto a microSD card. The screen shows where you and your friends are with no internet at all.

    Uses: LoRa long range radio, Mesh messaging, GPS, Screen. Add: microSD card.

  • Friend finder for groups

    Each node shares its GPS spot over the mesh. Everyone can see where the rest of the group is standing.

    Uses: LoRa long range radio, Mesh messaging, GPS.

  • Family storm check-in

    Keep a charged Tap in the house. Family members can post a quick status during a power or phone outage.

    Uses: LoRa long range radio, Mesh messaging, Battery ready.

  • Festival meetup screen

    Share your position every few minutes at a big outdoor event. Friends find your camp on their own screens or phones.

    Uses: LoRa long range radio, Mesh messaging, GPS, Screen.

  • Classroom radio range lesson

    Students carry nodes across the school grounds and note where messages arrive. They learn how walls and hills block radio waves.

    Uses: LoRa long range radio, Mesh messaging, GPS.

  • Phone app pairing

    Pair the Tap with the Meshtastic phone app over Bluetooth. You can type long messages on the phone and still see them on the device.

    Uses: Bluetooth, LoRa long range radio, Mesh messaging.

Builder ideas

  • Bird count field reports

    Volunteers on a nature survey send short sightings over the mesh. A leader at base gathers every report with its GPS spot.

    Uses: LoRa long range radio, Mesh messaging, GPS.

  • Community fair volunteer radio

    Hand out nodes to helpers at a fair or farmers market. Everyone shares updates across a field with no Wi-Fi.

    Uses: LoRa long range radio, Mesh messaging, Battery ready.

  • Solar powered camp node

    Plug a small 5 volt solar panel into the base board. The Tap keeps charging at a campsite for days.

    Uses: Battery ready, LoRa long range radio, Mesh messaging. Add: small 5 volt solar panel.

  • Coverage mapping walks

    Log GPS spots and signal strength on a walk around town. You end up with a map of where your local mesh reaches.

    Uses: LoRa long range radio, GPS, Mesh messaging.

  • Better antenna range test

    Swap the blade antenna for a taller one with an SMA plug. Compare how far your messages travel before and after.

    Uses: LoRa long range radio, GPS. Add: SMA LoRa antenna.

Expert ideas

  • Step and motion logger

    Build custom firmware that reads the onboard motion sensor. Show how much you moved on the color screen.

    Uses: Sensors built in, Screen.

  • Custom touchscreen layout

    Build the Meshtastic firmware in PlatformIO and change the screen design. Add a big clock or a battery gauge to the home page.

    Uses: Screen, Wi-Fi.
