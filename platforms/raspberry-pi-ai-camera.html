Raspberry Pi AI Camera

Raspberry Pi Ltd

A small Raspberry Pi camera that runs AI vision right on its own sensor.

Field marks: A 25 mm camera board on a 200 mm ribbon cable, running object detection on its own sensor with a spare RP2040 aboard to manage the models.

Overview

The Raspberry Pi AI Camera is a camera module with a Sony IMX500 sensor. The sensor has its own AI chip and runs vision models before the picture even reaches the Pi. A small RP2040 chip on the board loads and stores those models. It suits makers, teachers, and students who want smart camera projects on almost any Raspberry Pi.

How the scores work

Good fit or not?

Good for

  • Object detection and pose tracking on live video
  • Smart cameras that leave the Pi free for other jobs
  • Small builds on Raspberry Pi Zero boards
  • Learning AI vision with ready-made demos

Not great for

  • Fast motion shots, as the sensor uses a rolling shutter
  • Changing focus on the fly, as focus is set by hand
  • Beginners who want custom models, as converting them takes several tools
  • Use on its own, as it needs a Raspberry Pi to work

Specs in plain words

  • 12.3 megapixels gives sharp still photos, plenty for prints or close-up nature shots.
  • Video runs at 1080p and 30 frames per second, the same as a typical webcam.
  • A 78 degree view covers most of a small room from a corner.
  • 16 MB of flash on the board stores your recent AI models for quick reloads.
  • The module is about 2.5 centimeters square, roughly the size of a postage stamp.
  • A 20 centimeter ribbon cable comes in the box.

How open is it?

Openness level

Open CodeOpen BoardWild Open

Level 1 of 3. Wild Open shares the most.

  • Board plansNot published
  • Hardware licenseNot confirmed
  • Coding toolsMostly open
  • The chip itselfClosed chip design

The host software is open. Picamera2 and rpicam-apps use the BSD-2-Clause license, and the Sony model compression toolkit uses Apache-2.0. No schematics or board layout are published, and the sensor firmware and model converter stay closed.
