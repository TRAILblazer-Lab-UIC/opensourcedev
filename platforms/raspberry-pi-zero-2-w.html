Overview

Raspberry Pi Zero 2 W is a tiny Linux computer with Wi-Fi and Bluetooth. It is about the size of a stick of gum and lists for $15. It suits makers who want a full computer hidden inside a camera, game handheld or gadget. It works best without a desktop, run from another computer over Wi-Fi.

Good fit or not?

Good for

  • Small camera projects like bird and doorbell cams
  • Pocket game handhelds and tiny media players
  • Wi-Fi gadgets that tuck out of sight
  • Low-cost Linux learning for makers who like small boards

Not great for

  • Everyday desktop use, as 512 MB of memory is tight
  • Big AI or voice projects
  • Wired network projects, as it has no Ethernet port
  • Easy buying right now, as shops often sell out

Specs in plain words

  • Four cores at 1 GHz give it enough speed for cameras, small servers and classic games.
  • 512 MB of memory fits one or two light programs at a time.
  • It has no built-in storage, and the operating system lives on a microSD card.
  • The board measures 65 by 30 mm, about the size of a stick of gum.
  • One mini HDMI port and one micro USB data port need small adapters for normal cables.
  • A 5 volt, 2.5 amp micro USB power supply keeps it steady.

RadiosWi-Fi, Bluetooth Low Energy, Bluetooth Classic
ConnectorsMicro USB, Pin headers, Camera connector, microSD slot
Power5 V 2.5 A over micro USB
Size65 x 30 mm

There is no onboard eMMC. It boots from microSD. The 40-pin header is an unpopulated footprint on the Zero 2 W and is pre-soldered on the Zero 2 WH. gpio counts GPIO2 to GPIO27 on the 3.3 V header. There is no ADC. The single micro USB OTG port works as device or host, and a second micro USB port is for power only. Composite video and reset are on solder test points. Operating temperature is -20 to 70 C.
