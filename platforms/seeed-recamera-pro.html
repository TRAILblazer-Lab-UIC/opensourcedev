Overview

The reCamera Pro is an all-in-one AI camera from Seeed Studio. It packs an 8 megapixel camera, a small AI chip, two microphones, and a speaker in one cube. It can spot objects, turn speech into text, and talk back on the device itself. It suits makers and tinkerers who want a camera project with voice and vision in one box.

Good fit or not?

Good for

  • Smart security cameras that only alert on real events
  • Voice and vision helpers that work offline
  • Spotting objects with ready-made YOLO models
  • Factory or bench inspection tasks
  • Installs that use one network cable for power and data

Not great for

  • First-time builders on a tight budget, as it costs about 300 US dollars
  • Projects that need many wires to sensors, as it has only two general pins
  • Fully open hardware fans, as the board files are not published
  • Shoppers in the US, as no third-party US store sells it yet

Specs in plain words

  • The 3 TOPS AI chip spots objects in live video about 40 times a second.
  • 2 GB or 4 GB of memory is enough for a small chat model or a vision model.
  • The 8 megapixel sensor records sharp 4K video at 30 frames a second.
  • 16 GB of built-in storage holds the system and a few models. A microSD card adds room for clips.
  • Power over Ethernet means one network cable can carry both power and data.
  • The cube is about 7 by 7 by 9 centimeters and weighs about as much as a can of soup.

ram_kb is the 2 GB base model. A 4 GB model also exists. flash_kb is the 16 GB eMMC. The store page lists 2-channel 4-lane MIPI CSI, which the wiki does not mention. Wi-Fi and Bluetooth versions conflict. CNX and the store say Wi-Fi 5 and BT 5.4. The wiki spec page says Wi-Fi 5.2 and BT 5.2. The store page says 12 V 2 A DC. The wiki and repo say 12 to 24 V. USB-C is listed as USB 3.0 full-function OTG. The weight is 324 g in a finished enclosure.

    Uses: Camera, AI chip, Battery ready. Add: 7.4 V battery pack, weatherproof box.

    Set it on a table during a club meeting. The microphones pick up voices and the camera writes down what was said.

    Uses: Hears sound, AI chip.

    Uses: Sensors built in, Camera, Makes sound.

Expert ideas

  • Talking desk helper

    Ask a question out loud. The camera turns your words into text, runs a small language model, and speaks the answer.

    Uses: Hears sound, Makes sound, AI chip, Runs Linux.

  • What am I holding game

    Hold up an object and ask what it is. A small vision model looks and says the answer through the speaker.

    Uses: Camera, Makes sound, AI chip.

How open is it?

Openness level

Open CodeOpen BoardWild Open

Level 1 of 3. Wild Open shares the most.

  • Board plansNot published
  • Hardware licenseNot confirmed
  • Coding toolsMostly open
  • The chip itselfClosed chip design

The Linux system uses the GPL kernel and U-Boot, and Seeed's extension API is Apache-2.0. The AI runtime, image tuning, and the Rockchip chip itself stay closed. Seeed has not published schematics or board files for this model yet.

Hardware license: none for Pro hardware (repo is Apache-2.0 but holds only images and specs for the Pro) · Software license: GPL-2.0 kernel and U-Boot, Apache-2.0 and MIT media/mpp, Apache-2.0 ext-api, RKNN runtime, RockIVA and ISP tuning are Rockchip proprietary
