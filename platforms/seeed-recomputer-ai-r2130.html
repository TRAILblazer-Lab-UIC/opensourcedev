Overview

The reComputer AI R2130 is a Raspberry Pi 5 with 8 GB of memory in a fan-cooled metal case. Inside sits a Hailo-8 AI card that speeds up image tasks a lot. A second slot holds a fast SSD for storage. It suits people who want a ready Pi AI box for camera projects without building one from parts.

Good fit or not?

Good for

  • Camera recorders with people, car, and pet alerts
  • Running YOLO object detection on live video
  • Learning edge AI on familiar Raspberry Pi OS
  • Long-running projects that need cooling and a solid case

Not great for

  • Wiring sensors, as the GPIO pins sit inside the case
  • Small budgets, as it costs around 300 to 370 US dollars
  • Total beginners, as setup needs a config file change for the AI slot
  • Large chat models, as 8 GB of memory limits model size

Specs in plain words

  • The 26 TOPS AI chip runs object detection on video many times faster than the Pi alone.
  • The Pi 5 processor runs at 2.4 GHz and feels like a modest desktop computer.
  • 8 GB of memory lets you run a web browser, a camera app, and an AI model at once.
  • The two M.2 slots hold the AI card and an SSD for fast storage.
  • It draws about 6 watts at rest and up to 16 watts at full work, a bit more than a phone charger.
  • The case is about the size of a deck of cards stacked twice.

Size95 x 63 mm

Size is the case, 95 x 63 x 45 mm. It boots from microSD by default and can boot from an NVMe SSD in the second M.2 slot. As a result, flash_kb is null. Video out is two micro HDMI ports. The FAQ says the Pi 40-pin GPIO header is inside the case, with no external GPIO. As a result, gpio is left null. The R2140-12 is the same box with 16 GB RAM. The getting started page needs a pcie-fix device tree overlay for the M.2 slots. Seeed measured 5.9 W idle and 16.2 W at full load with the Hailo-8 at 81 C.

Level 1 of 3. Wild Open shares the most.

The software is mostly open, with Raspberry Pi OS, the Hailo runtime, and Seeed's MIT tutorials. The Hailo model compiler, the Pi boot firmware, and the chips stay closed. Seeed has not published schematics for the box or its M.2 board.
