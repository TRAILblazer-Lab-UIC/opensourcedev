Overview

The SenseCAP Card Tracker T1000-E is a thin, sealed tracker about the size of a credit card. It has LoRa radio, GPS, and Bluetooth inside an IP65 case. It ships ready for Meshtastic and pairs with a phone in minutes. Hikers, families, and event groups can use it with no setup skills.

Good fit or not?

Good for

  • Off-grid texting with a phone over Bluetooth
  • Sharing locations in a group with no cell service
  • Carrying in a wallet or pocket in wet weather
  • First steps into Meshtastic for newcomers

Not great for

  • Adding sensors or wires, as the case has no headers
  • Long trips with no charger, as the battery lasts about two days in Meshtastic use
  • Talking directly with older SX127x LoRa radios
  • Charging with any cable, as it needs its own magnetic cable

Specs in plain words

  • It measures 85 by 55 by 6.5 mm. That is about the size of a credit card and as thin as a few stacked coins.
  • IP65 means it shrugs off dust and rain splashes.
  • The 700 mAh battery is small. Expect to recharge it every day or two.
  • The LoRa radio uses the licence-free 863 to 928 MHz bands. Pick the region setting for your country.
  • The GPS chip finds your spot outdoors and shares it over the mesh.
  • The nRF52840 chip runs at 64 MHz with 256 KB of working memory. That is plenty for messaging and tracking.

Sealed device with no user GPIO or headers. The four pogo pins carry USB, DFU, serial logging and charging. As a result, connectors is empty. The magnetic pogo cable is not in the vocab. Onboard buzzer and single LED are not in the onboard vocab. Meshtastic does not yet use the light sensor or accelerometer. The LR1110 also has Wi-Fi and GNSS scanning features, which Meshtastic does not use. CNX lists the LR1110 range as 150 to 960 MHz. The device is sold for 863 to 928 MHz. LoRa TX power follows region limits (US 30 dBm per the Seeed wiki table). Sleep current was not found.
