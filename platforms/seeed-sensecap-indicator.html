Seeed SenseCAP Indicator

A 4 inch touchscreen desk panel for smart home dashboards and LoRa mesh messages.

Overview

The Seeed SenseCAP Indicator is a square 4 inch touchscreen in a desk-stand case. Two chips share the work. An ESP32-S3 runs Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, and the screen, and an RP2040 handles add-ons. Some models add a LoRa radio for Meshtastic, and the D1Pro adds air quality sensors. It suits smart home fans and Meshtastic users who want a big screen at their desk.

Good fit or not?

Good for

  • Home Assistant and ESPHome dashboards
  • A Meshtastic node with a big screen on a desk
  • Showing air quality on the D1Pro model
  • Plug-in sensors through two Grove ports
  • Low cost for a large touchscreen

Not great for

  • Carrying around, as it has no battery
  • Meshtastic on the base D1 model, as only the D1L and D1Pro have LoRa
  • Buzzer, microSD, and maps under Meshtastic, as that firmware does not support them
  • Beginners porting new drivers, as many radio pins hide behind an extra chip

Specs in plain words

  • The ESP32-S3 chip runs up to 240 MHz. It is quick enough to draw a smooth touchscreen.
  • 8 MB of storage holds the firmware plus screen pictures and fonts.
  • The 480 by 480 screen is about the size of a coaster.
  • The optional LoRa radio covers 862 to 930 MHz. Set it to the licence-free band your country allows.
  • Power comes from a 5 volt USB-C cable. Plan a spot near an outlet.
  • The D1Pro carbon dioxide sensor sits near a small heater. Its temperature reading runs a little warm.

Ways to connect partsI2C, SPI, Serial (UART), USB device, Screen bus

ConnectorsUSB-C, Grove, microSD slot
Built inscreen, buttons, LoRa radio
Power5 V 1 A over USB-C. No battery

cores, clock and RAM describe the ESP32-S3. The RP2040 runs dual Cortex-M0+ cores at up to 133 MHz with 2 MB external flash (W25Q16). ESP32-S3 flash is an external 8 MB W25Q64 per the schematic. ESPHome runs PSRAM in octal mode at 80 MHz, but no page read stated the PSRAM size. LoRa is fitted only on the D1L and D1Pro. The CO2 (SCD41) and tVOC (SGP40) sensors on the D1S and D1Pro are not in the onboard vocab. The D1S and D1Pro also ship a Grove AHT20 temperature and humidity sensor. The buzzer is an MLT-8530. One USB-C port exposes internal GPIO pins. Dimensions were shown only in images.

    Set a D1Pro on the teacher's desk. The screen shows carbon dioxide levels and reminds everyone to open a window.

    Uses: Screen, Wi-Fi.

    Leave the panel plugged in at a community center. Visitors read local mesh news on the big screen.

    Uses: LoRa long range radio, Mesh messaging, Screen. Add: LoRa antenna.

  • Phone pairing for mesh texts

    Pair the panel with the Meshtastic phone app over Bluetooth. Type on your phone and watch messages show on the big screen.

    Uses: Bluetooth, LoRa long range radio, Mesh messaging.

Level 2 of 3. Wild Open shares the most.

Seeed publishes a full schematic PDF and a 3D model of the case. The ESP32 SDK uses the Apache-2.0 license, and the RP2040 examples and Meshtastic firmware use GPL-3.0. No board layout files or hardware license are public. The ESP32-S3 chip and its Wi-Fi and Bluetooth code stay closed.
