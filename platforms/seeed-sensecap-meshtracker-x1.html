Overview

The SenseCAP MeshTracker X1 is a sealed pocket radio from Seeed Studio. It sends short text messages and GPS spots over LoRa radio with no cell signal. It pairs with your phone over Bluetooth and runs Meshtastic or MeshCore. It suits hikers, campers, event groups, and anyone new to off-grid messaging.

Specs in plain words

  • LoRa radio reaches up to about 8 kilometers in open country, roughly five miles.
  • Dual-band GPS finds your spot more reliably near tall buildings and trees.
  • The 1100 mAh battery lasts up to five days between charges.
  • The barometer reads air pressure, which hints at height and coming weather.
  • IP66 means the case handles rain and dust on the trail.
  • It is about the size of a credit card and weighs about as much as a few coins.
Full spec sheet
ProcessorNordic nRF52840, Arm Cortex-M4F, 1 core at 64 MHz
Memory256 KB
Storage1 MB
Ways to connect partsUSB device
RadiosBluetooth Low Energy, LoRa, GPS
ConnectorsUSB-C
Built inGPS, LoRa radio, temperature sensor, buttons, color LED, battery charger
Power5 V over USB-C. Internal 1100 mAh rechargeable battery
Size90 x 57 x 8 mm

Sealed device with no user GPIO or headers. Clock, RAM and flash are the standard nRF52840 figures. The wiki also lists 8 MB of external SPI flash. The barometer (300 to 1100 hPa), buzzer and vibration motor are not in the onboard vocab. Seeed says a later model may add a 3-axis sensor and magnetometer. Operating range is -20 to 60 C and charging works from 5 to 45 C. Range claim is 8 km line of sight at 915 MHz.
