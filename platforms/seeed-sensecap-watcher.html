Overview

The SenseCAP Watcher is a small AI camera and voice assistant from Seeed Studio. You give it a job in plain words through the SenseCraft app. It watches a room and reacts to what it sees and hears. Two chips share the work, with one for Wi-Fi and the screen and one for vision.

Good fit or not?

Good for

  • People and pet alerts with no code at all
  • Smart home fans who use Home Assistant or Node-RED
  • Voice chat projects with the XiaoZhi edition
  • Makers who want a finished device with a case and screen

Not great for

  • Heavy AI on the device alone, as big questions go to a cloud model or a local server
  • Swapping between vision and voice, as each edition is sold separately
  • Projects that need many free pins, as the header has only two spare ones
  • Full hardware remixes, as only the schematic is shared

Specs in plain words

  • The 1.45 inch round touchscreen is about the size of a large coin.
  • The 5 MP camera sees a wide 120 degree view, enough for most of a small room.
  • The Himax chip has a small AI engine that spots people and objects right on the device.
  • The 8 MB of extra memory and 32 MB of storage hold the screen graphics, audio, and firmware.
  • The 400 mAh battery is a backup that keeps it alive through a short unplug.
  • The whole box measures about 69 by 65 by 20 mm, close to a deck of cards laid flat.
Full spec sheet
ProcessorXtensa LX7 (ESP32-S3) plus Arm Cortex-M55 (Himax HX6538), 2 cores at 240 MHz
Memory512 KB plus 8 MB extra (PSRAM)
Storage32 MB
AI chipArm Ethos-U55 microNPU in the Himax HX6538
Pins2 pins
Ways to connect partsI2C, SPI, Digital audio (I2S), USB device, Camera port (MIPI CSI), SD card bus
RadiosWi-Fi, Bluetooth Low Energy
ConnectorsUSB-C, Grove, Pin headers, microSD slot, Battery plug
Built incamera, microphone, speaker, screen, buttons, color LED, battery charger
Power5 V over USB-C
Size69 x 65 x 20 mm

Core, cores, clock, RAM, PSRAM and flash describe the ESP32-S3 main MCU. The Himax HX6538 has its own 16 MB flash. GPIO counts only the two free GPIO on the 2 x 4 header, which also carries I2C and power. The wiki says the camera is likely on MIPI and the screen on SPI and I2C. The microSD slot is listed as SPI. One USB-C port on the back is power only. The bottom port powers and programs the device. No NPU TOPS figure was stated on the pages read.

Things you could build

14 ideas sized to what this board can do. Starter ideas suit first-time builders.

Starter ideas

  • Pet on the couch alert

    Tell the Watcher to look for a dog on the sofa. It sends a message to your phone the moment it sees one.

    Uses: Camera, Wi-Fi.

  • Front door person spotter

    Point it at the hallway and ask it to watch for people. It beeps and logs each visitor.

    Uses: Camera, Makes sound.

  • Talking desk buddy

    Run the XiaoZhi voice firmware and chat with it out loud. It answers questions through its small speaker.

    Uses: Hears sound, Makes sound, Wi-Fi.

  • Bird feeder visit counter

    Set it near a window feeder with a task to spot birds. It keeps a tally of visits through the day.

    Uses: Camera, Wi-Fi.

Builder ideas

  • Home Assistant room sensor

    Link the Watcher to Home Assistant. Lights turn on as soon as it sees someone walk in.

    Uses: Camera, Wi-Fi.

  • Classroom seat counter

    Mount it high in a study room to count people. Teachers see how full the room gets each hour.

    Uses: Camera, Wi-Fi, Screen. Add: small wall mount.

  • Plant check reminder

    Ask it to watch a houseplant for drooping leaves. It shows a reminder on the round screen.

    Uses: Camera, Screen.

  • Garden gate watcher

    Put it in a covered spot facing the gate. The backup battery keeps it running through short power cuts.

    Uses: Camera, Battery ready, Wi-Fi. Add: weatherproof box.

  • Room air and light logger

    Plug a Grove sensor into the side port. The screen shows temperature and humidity next to the camera view.

    Uses: Plug-in connectors, Screen. Add: temperature and humidity sensor.

  • Node-RED alert flow

    Send the Watcher's detections into Node-RED. Build a flow that emails you a snapshot.

    Uses: Camera, Wi-Fi.

  • Science fair motion study

    Record how often a hamster wheel spins each night. Save the logs to a microSD card for a graph.

    Uses: Camera. Add: microSD card.

Expert ideas

  • Voice-controlled lamp

    Use XiaoZhi device control to switch a relay by voice. Say a command and the lamp turns on.

    Uses: Hears sound, Wi-Fi, Plug-in connectors. Add: relay module, lamp.

  • Private local AI server

    Point SenseCraft tasks at your own local server. Your camera images stay inside your home network.

    Uses: Camera, Wi-Fi. Add: spare computer.

  • Custom vision model

    Train a small model to spot one object, such as a toy car. Flash it to the Himax chip and run it on the device.

    Uses: Camera.

Seeed shares the v1.0 schematic and a 3D model of the shell under the Apache-2.0 license. The ESP32-S3 firmware SDK is Apache-2.0, and the XiaoZhi firmware is MIT. The Himax chip firmware ships as a closed image, and the SenseCraft cloud service is closed.
