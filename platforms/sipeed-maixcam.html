Overview

The MaixCAM is a small AI camera kit from Sipeed. It comes with a camera, a little touchscreen, a microphone, Wi-Fi, and Bluetooth in a printed case. A built-in AI helper chip runs object and face detection right on the device. Beginners can write short Python scripts in the free MaixVision app and see results on the screen.

Good fit or not?

Good for

  • Learning how AI vision works with real objects and faces
  • Classroom projects that count, sort, or spot things
  • Garden and wildlife cameras that save photos on their own
  • Robots that need eyes
  • Trying ready-made apps from the MaixHub store

Not great for

  • Big AI chat models, which need more memory
  • Running for days on a battery, with no battery built in
  • Projects that need a fully open board design
  • Shoppers in the US, who will find few local stores

Specs in plain words

  • The 1 TOPS AI chip can check a live video for objects many times each second.
  • 256 MB of memory is enough for small vision models and a simple app.
  • The 4 MP camera takes photos about as sharp as an older phone.
  • The 2.3 inch touchscreen is about the size of a large postage stamp.
  • Wi-Fi 6 and Bluetooth let it send pictures and messages to phones and laptops.
  • Two rows of header pins connect sensors, lights, and motors.

cores counts the Linux core (C906 or A53, chosen at boot) and the 700 MHz RTOS core. The 8051 low-power core is not counted. flash_kb is null because the board boots from a microSD card or optional SD NAND. No eMMC is fitted. The GPIO count is not stated. The two 14-pin headers carry power and I/O. An Ethernet version exists, but the standard kit has Wi-Fi 6 and BLE 5.4 only. The board has a speaker amplifier output for up to 1 W. A speaker is not listed as onboard. A Lite version without screen and case was sold at launch.

How open is it?

Openness level

Open CodeOpen BoardWild Open

Level 2 of 3. Wild Open shares the most.

  • Board plansSchematics only
  • Hardware licenseNot confirmed
  • Coding toolsMostly open
  • The chip itselfClosed chip design

The MaixPy and MaixCDK software are open under the Apache-2.0 license. Sipeed shares schematics for the core board inside the kit, but the full board design is missing. The main chip is closed, and some camera, AI, and Wi-Fi drivers come as closed files.
