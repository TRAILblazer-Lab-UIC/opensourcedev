Sipeed MaixCAM2

Sipeed

A palm-sized AI camera that runs Linux and small chat models offline.

Overview

The MaixCAM2 is the second AI camera kit from Sipeed. It has a 4K-ready camera input, a touchscreen, two microphones, a speaker, a motion sensor, Wi-Fi, and Bluetooth. Its AI chip is about three times faster than the first MaixCAM. The 4 GB version can even run small models that describe pictures with no internet.

Specs in plain words

  • The 3.2 TOPS AI chip can label objects in live video about a hundred times each second.
  • 1 GB or 4 GB of memory decides how big an AI model it can run. The 4 GB version shows about 2 GB at first until you change one setting.
  • 32 GB of built-in storage holds the system and thousands of photos.
  • The 2.4 inch touchscreen is about the size of a matchbox.
  • The bare board is a bit smaller than a credit card.
  • The PMOD port has 20 pins for sensors, motors and camera add-ons.

ram_kb is the 1 GB base model. A 4 GB model is sold, and Sipeed's FAQ says Linux sees about 2 GB of it by default because the rest is reserved for media and NPU memory. flash_kb is the 32 GB eMMC. Some Amazon listings also show 64 GB eMMC and no-eMMC versions. ai_tops uses the INT8 figure. cores counts the two A53 cores. The E907 real-time core is not counted. gpio is the 20 I/Os on the PMOD header. The core board exposes more. Ethernet needs an external FPC-to-RJ45 module. Size is the bare board without lens. The case is 65 x 49 x 20 mm with a 1/4 inch tripod mount. CNX Software reports about 2.5 W while running Qwen3-VL-2B.

Starter ideas

  • Pet spotter with a voice

    The camera watches the back door for your cat or dog. The speaker announces each visit.

    Uses: Camera, AI chip, Makes sound.

  • Classroom object quiz

    Students hold up objects in front of the lens. The screen names each one and the speaker reads it aloud.

    Uses: Camera, AI chip, Screen, Makes sound.

  • 4K time-lapse of a sunset

    Take one sharp photo every minute from a window. Join them into a short movie of the sky changing color.

    Uses: Camera, Runs Linux. Add: small tripod.

  • Live garden stream

    Stream video over Wi-Fi to a laptop. You can watch your vegetable beds from inside the house.

    Uses: Camera, Wi-Fi.

  • Sound level watcher

    The two microphones measure noise in a library or classroom. The screen turns red when it gets too loud.

    Uses: Hears sound, Screen.

  • Tilt-controlled drawing toy

    Tilt the camera box to steer a line around the touchscreen. The motion sensor reads each tilt.

    Uses: Sensors built in, Screen.

Builder ideas

  • Bird feeder visitor log

    Aim the camera at a garden feeder. It saves a photo and the time of each bird visit.

    Uses: Camera, AI chip. Add: outdoor weather cover.

  • Battery-powered trail camera

    Clip on a battery and leave the camera by a garden path. It records any animal that walks by.

    Uses: Camera, AI chip, Battery ready. Add: lithium-ion battery, outdoor weather cover.

  • Heat map of a leaky window

    Add a thermal camera module to the PMOD port. The screen shows cold drafts as blue patches.

    Uses: Screen. Add: thermal camera module.

  • Robot with eyes and balance

    Mount the camera on a small robot car. It follows a person and uses its motion sensor to sense bumps.

    Uses: Camera, AI chip, Sensors built in. Add: robot car kit, motor driver board.

  • Depth-sensing door counter

    Add a distance camera module above a doorway. It counts people walking in and out.

    Uses: AI chip. Add: time-of-flight depth camera module.

  • Exercise rep counter

    Pose tracking watches your arms during squats or jumping jacks. The speaker counts each rep out loud.

    Uses: Camera, AI chip, Makes sound.

  • Wired security camera

    Connect a network cable through the Ethernet adapter. The camera streams steady video with no Wi-Fi dropouts.

    Uses: Camera, Wired network. Add: Ethernet adapter module, network cable.

  • Smart recycling sorter

    Train a model on cans, bottles, and paper. A servo flap tips each item into the right bin.

    Uses: Camera, AI chip. Add: servo motor, cardboard bins, jumper wires.

  • Bluetooth gesture remote

    Wave your hand in front of the lens to skip songs. Bluetooth sends each command to a phone or computer.

    Uses: Camera, AI chip, Bluetooth.

Expert ideas

  • Ask the camera what it sees

    Run a small vision-language model on the 4 GB version. Ask a question out loud and hear a spoken answer about the scene.

    Uses: Camera, AI chip, Hears sound, Makes sound.

    Point the camera at a page of printed words. A small model reads the text and speaks it aloud.

    Uses: Camera, AI chip, Makes sound.
