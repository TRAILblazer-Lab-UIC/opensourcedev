Overview

The expLoRaBLE is a Feather-sized SparkFun board with a LoRa radio and Bluetooth on one chip. LoRa sends small messages over long distances on very little power. It suits makers who want battery sensors that report from far away. Its circuit design files are open.

Good fit or not?

Good for

  • Battery sensors across a farm, garden, or campus
  • LoRaWAN projects on The Things Network
  • Point-to-point radio links between two boards
  • Bluetooth projects that talk to a phone
  • Plug-in sensors through the Qwiic connector with no soldering

Not great for

  • Wi-Fi projects, as the board has no Wi-Fi
  • Complete beginners, as setup has a few traps
  • Ready-made mesh chat apps, with Meshtastic support unconfirmed
  • Reading 3.3 volt analog signals, as the analog inputs top out at 2 volts

Specs in plain words

  • LoRa can send short messages across a neighborhood or more with the right antenna.
  • Bluetooth Low Energy lets the board chat with a phone nearby.
  • 1 MB of flash and 384 KB of memory hold a sensor program with plenty of room left.
  • The Apollo3 chip sips power, which helps a small battery last a long time.
  • The built-in charger refills a single-cell LiPo battery over USB-C.
  • The board is about 58 by 23 mm, close to the size of a stick of gum.

Size is from the stated 2.3 x 0.9 inches. ADC is 14-bit with a 0 to 2 V input range. As a result, 3.3 V signals saturate it. All 21 digital pins can do 16-bit PWM. USB serial uses a CH340E bridge. Charger is an MCP73831 set to 500 mA. Onboard LEDs are PWR, CHG, RX, TX and a blue user LED on pin 18. Buttons are reset and user pin 10. The u.FL antenna jack has no connector vocab entry. Sleep current was not found.

Things you could build

12 ideas sized to what this board can do. Starter ideas suit first-time builders.

Starter ideas

  • Mailbox alert

    Put a door switch on your mailbox and send a LoRa message on each opening. A second board in the house blinks a light.

    Uses: LoRa long range radio, Battery ready. Add: magnetic door switch, small LiPo battery, antenna, second LoRa board.

  • Phone-readable thermometer

    Plug in a Qwiic temperature sensor and share readings over Bluetooth. A phone app shows the room temperature.

    Uses: Bluetooth, Plug-in connectors. Add: temperature sensor with Qwiic cable.

  • Bluetooth plant reminder

    Check soil moisture and light a blue LED on a dry day. A phone can also read the value over Bluetooth.

    Uses: Bluetooth. Add: soil moisture sensor.

  • Classroom radio range test

    Students walk away from a base board and log signal strength. They map how far LoRa reaches around the school.

    Uses: LoRa long range radio, Battery ready. Add: antenna, LiPo battery, second board.

Builder ideas

  • Garden soil monitor

    Read a soil sensor at the far end of the garden and send the value home by radio. You know which bed needs water.

    Uses: LoRa long range radio, Battery ready. Add: soil moisture sensor, LiPo battery, antenna, weatherproof box.

  • Community weather node

    Send temperature and humidity to The Things Network over LoRaWAN. Neighbors can view local weather on a shared map.

    Uses: LoRa long range radio, Plug-in connectors. Add: temperature and humidity sensor, antenna, LiPo battery.

  • Two-way text pagers

    Build two handheld boards that send short preset messages across a park. Friends stay in touch without cell service.

    Uses: LoRa long range radio, Battery ready. Add: two antennas, two LiPo batteries, small buttons, second board.

  • Trail counter

    Mount a motion sensor beside a trail and send a daily count by radio. Park staff see how busy the path gets.

    Uses: LoRa long range radio, Battery ready. Add: motion sensor, LiPo battery, antenna, weatherproof box.

  • Beehive weight logger

    Weigh a hive with a load cell and send the weight by LoRa each hour. Beekeepers see honey stores grow from far away.

    Uses: LoRa long range radio, Plug-in connectors. Add: load cell with Qwiic amplifier, antenna, LiPo battery.

  • Water tank level alert

    Measure a rain barrel level with a distance sensor. The board sends a LoRa alert as the barrel runs low.

    Uses: LoRa long range radio, Plug-in connectors. Add: distance sensor with Qwiic cable, antenna, LiPo battery.

  • Freezer door watchdog

    Sense a freezer door left open and send a radio alert. Food stays safe in a garage or basement.

    Uses: LoRa long range radio, Battery ready. Add: magnetic door switch, temperature sensor, antenna.

Expert ideas

  • Small relay network

    Pass messages from board to board to reach farther than one radio can. Each board repeats what it hears.

    Uses: LoRa long range radio, Mesh messaging. Add: several boards, antennas, batteries.
