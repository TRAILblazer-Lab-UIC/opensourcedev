Overview

The TinyWATCH S3 is a do-it-yourself smartwatch from Unexpected Maker in Australia. It runs on an ESP32-S3 chip with Wi-Fi and Bluetooth LE. A color touchscreen sits on top of motion sensors, a magnetometer, a microphone, a buzzer, and a haptic motor. It suits makers who want to write their own watch faces and wearable apps in C++.

Good fit or not?

Good for

  • Writing custom watch faces and small wrist apps
  • Learning wearable firmware with real sensors
  • Wi-Fi and Bluetooth gadgets you wear
  • Studying a fully open smartwatch design

Not great for

  • Newcomers who want a finished watch
  • Buyers in the United States, where the store does not ship
  • People who want polished software, as the firmware is early pre-alpha
  • Projects that need extra pins, as every pin already drives a built-in part

Specs in plain words

  • The dual-core 240 MHz chip can run the screen, sensors, and radio at the same time.
  • 2 MB of PSRAM gives room for smooth graphics and watch faces.
  • 8 MB of flash holds the firmware, with a second flash chip for your data.
  • The 240 by 280 touchscreen shows color graphics and responds to taps.
  • The low-power clock chip keeps time even with the main chip asleep.
  • The fuel gauge reports how much battery is left.
Full spec sheet
ProcessorXtensa LX7 (ESP32-S3), 2 cores at 240 MHz
Memory512 KB plus 2 MB extra (PSRAM)
Storage8 MB
Ways to connect partsI2C, SPI, Digital audio (I2S), USB device
RadiosWi-Fi, Bluetooth Low Energy
ConnectorsUSB-C, Battery plug
Built inscreen, motion sensor, microphone, speaker, buttons, battery charger
Power5 V over USB-C or a single-cell LiPo

Specs come from CNX Software and the Lectronz listing for revision P10. The speaker entry is a magnetic buzzer. The battery is not included, and Lectronz says it fits a LiPo up to the 602530 size. By contrast, CNX mentions 250 or 500 mAh cells. All ESP32-S3 pins drive internal parts, and the watch has no pin headers. A 700 mA 3.3 V LDO powers the board. The repo did not state the outer case size or sleep current.

Builder ideas

  • Design a custom watch face

    Draw your own clock face with colors and fonts on the 240 by 280 touchscreen. Wear something nobody else has.

    Uses: Screen. Add: small LiPo battery, 20 mm watch strap.

  • Step counter

    Use the motion sensor to count steps through the day. Show the total on the screen.

    Uses: Sensors built in, Screen, Battery ready. Add: small LiPo battery, watch strap.

  • Gentle buzz reminders

    Set quiet buzzes to remind you to drink water or stretch. The haptic motor taps your wrist.

    Uses: Battery ready, Screen. Add: small LiPo battery, watch strap.

  • Weather on your wrist

    Pull the local forecast over Wi-Fi and show it next to the time. It uses a free weather service key.

    Uses: Wi-Fi, Screen. Add: small LiPo battery.

  • Digital compass

    Read the magnetometer and draw a compass needle on screen. Take it on a hike.

    Uses: Sensors built in, Screen. Add: small LiPo battery, watch strap.

  • Classroom noise meter

    Use the microphone to show a sound level bar on the screen. Students can see how loud the room gets.

    Uses: Hears sound, Screen. Add: small LiPo battery.

  • Pocket metronome

    Tap out a steady beat with the buzzer and the haptic motor. Musicians can feel the tempo on their wrist.

    Uses: Makes sound, Screen. Add: small LiPo battery, watch strap.

  • Tap rhythm game

    Build a small game where you tap the screen in time with buzzer beats. Track high scores on the watch.

    Uses: Screen, Makes sound. Add: small LiPo battery.

Expert ideas

  • Phone notification display

    Send short alerts from your phone to the watch over Bluetooth LE. Read them with a glance.

    Uses: Bluetooth, Screen. Add: small LiPo battery.

  • Gesture remote control

    Flick your wrist to send commands over Bluetooth LE. Use it to change slides or control a game.

    Uses: Sensors built in, Bluetooth. Add: small LiPo battery, watch strap.

  • Sleep motion logger

    Log wrist movement through the night to the onboard flash. Graph the data on your computer in the morning.

    Uses: Sensors built in, Battery ready. Add: small LiPo battery, watch strap.

  • Garden sensor dashboard

    Read soil and temperature data from a Wi-Fi garden station. Check your plants from your wrist.

    Uses: Wi-Fi, Screen. Add: small LiPo battery, separate Wi-Fi sensor board, soil moisture sensor.
