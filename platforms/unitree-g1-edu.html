Overview

The Unitree G1 EDU is the research version of Unitree's G1 humanoid robot. It stands about 1.32 meters tall, weighs about 35 kilograms, and has 23 to 43 joint motors. It carries a depth camera, a 3D LiDAR, microphones, and an optional NVIDIA Jetson Orin module. University labs use it for walking, teleoperation, and imitation learning research.

Good fit or not?

Good for

  • University research on walking and balance
  • Training robot skills in simulation and moving them to real hardware
  • Teleoperation and learning from human demonstration
  • Graduate robotics courses with ROS 2

Not great for

  • Beginners, hobbyists, or K-12 classrooms
  • Small budgets, with the EDU Standard listed above 42,000 dollars
  • Long sessions, with about two hours per battery
  • Anyone who wants open hardware design files

Specs in plain words

  • Up to 43 joint motors let it bend at the legs, waist, arms, wrists, and fingers.
  • A knee torque of up to 120 Nm is enough to hold up its own weight through squats and steps.
  • The optional Jetson Orin module adds about 100 TOPS of AI power for vision and learning models.
  • The 9,000 mAh swappable battery runs it for about one and a half to two hours.
  • Wi-Fi 6, Bluetooth 5.2, and Ethernet connect it to your lab computers.
  • The EDU arms lift about 3 kilograms, about the weight of a large bag of flour.

Unitree lists 1320 x 450 x 200 mm standing, 690 x 450 x 300 mm folded and about 35 kg or more for the EDU. EDU legs have 6 DoF each, arms 5 DoF each, a 1 DoF waist plus 2 optional, and optional 2 DoF wrists and 7 DoF hands. Arm payload is about 3 kg on the EDU versus 2 kg on the base G1. Unitree names Orin only as an example optional module and does not name the CPU. ai_tops uses the Orin NX 100 TOPS value from RobotShop and an independent review. The IMU is inferred from the locomotion features and was not listed as a part.

Expert ideas

  • Walking gait research

    Train a new walking style in a robot simulator. Move the trained policy onto the real robot and test it on flat ground.

    Uses: Drives motors, Sensors built in, Runs Linux. Add: desktop computer with a strong graphics card.

  • Teleoperation with a headset

    Wear an XR headset and move your arms. The robot copies your motions in real time.

    Uses: Drives motors, Camera, Wi-Fi. Add: XR headset.

  • Teach by demonstration

    Record a person guiding the robot through a task. Train an imitation learning model to repeat it.

    Uses: Drives motors, Camera, AI chip, Runs Linux. Add: XR headset, dexterous hands.

  • Museum greeter

    Have the robot wave and speak a welcome through its speaker. Visitors hear a short talk about the exhibit.

    Uses: Drives motors, Makes sound, Runs Linux.

  • Voice command demo

    Listen through the four-microphone array for simple spoken commands. The robot answers with a matching gesture.

    Uses: Hears sound, Drives motors, AI chip.

  • Room mapping walk

    Walk the robot through a building with its 3D LiDAR running. Build a map that a later program can use.

    Uses: Sensors built in, Runs Linux, Wired network.

  • Object pickup study

    Use the depth camera to find a cup on a table. Reach for it and grasp it with the force-sensing hands.

    Uses: Camera, Drives motors, AI chip. Add: dexterous hands.

  • Fall recovery testing

    Train a get-up routine in simulation. Try it on the real robot over thick gym mats.

    Uses: Drives motors, Sensors built in, Runs Linux. Add: gym mats, safety gantry.

  • ROS 2 robotics course

    Teach a graduate class to read joint states and send commands through ROS 2. Students test code in MuJoCo first.

    Uses: Runs Linux, Wired network, Drives motors. Add: lab computers.

  • Person following

    Spot a person with the depth camera and AI module. The robot walks behind them at a safe distance.

    Uses: Camera, AI chip, Drives motors.

  • Robot network security lab

    Put the robot on its own isolated network. Study the Bluetooth and telemetry issues that researchers reported in 2025.

    Uses: Bluetooth, Wi-Fi, Wired network, Runs Linux. Add: separate network router.

  • Dance routine

    Script a short dance from joint positions and play music through the speaker. Show it off at a school open house.

    Uses: Drives motors, Makes sound.
