Overview

The Viam Rover 2 is a preassembled two-wheel rover made by Viam to teach its robotics software. It has an aluminum chassis, two motors with encoders, a webcam with a microphone, a motion sensor, and a power monitor. You add a Raspberry Pi 4 as the brain. Viam has stopped selling it, but the setup guides and open design files stay online.

Specs in plain words

  • The chassis carries up to 20 pounds, about the weight of a full laundry basket.
  • Encoders on the motors count wheel turns, which helps the rover drive straight and measure distance.
  • The 720p webcam gives clear video for driving and simple vision tasks.
  • The MPU6050 motion sensor feels tilts and bumps.
  • The INA219 power monitor reports how much battery you have used.
  • It runs on four 18650 cells or an RC battery pack.

The rover ships without a computer. Host values are for the recommended Raspberry Pi 4, whose RAM depends on the model bought. Wi-Fi and Bluetooth come from the Pi. The rover itself has no radio. The IMU (MPU6050) and power monitor (INA219) sit on I2C. The webcam connects over USB. The low-voltage cutoff defaults to 12 V, and the setup guide sets it to 14.7 V for a 4S pack. Dimensions, weight and motor specs were not stated on the fetched pages. They may be derivable from the STEP assembly.

Builder ideas

  • Drive it from a browser

    Load the ready-made config in the Viam app. Steer the rover from your laptop and watch the webcam feed.

    Uses: Wi-Fi, Camera, Drives motors, Runs Linux.

  • Square dance script

    Write a short Python script that drives in a square. Change the numbers to make other shapes.

    Uses: Drives motors, Wi-Fi, Runs Linux.

  • Color follower

    Teach the rover to spot a bright colored ball with the webcam. It turns and rolls toward it.

    Uses: Camera, Drives motors, Runs Linux. Add: brightly colored ball.

  • Tilt and bump logger

    Read the motion sensor as the rover drives over rugs and ramps. Graph the bumps for a science lesson.

    Uses: Sensors built in, Drives motors, Runs Linux.

  • Battery health monitor

    Read the built-in power monitor during a long drive. Learn how speed affects battery life.

    Uses: Sensors built in, Battery ready, Runs Linux.

  • Sound-activated rover

    Listen through the webcam microphone for a clap. The rover starts or stops on cue.

    Uses: Hears sound, Drives motors, Runs Linux.

  • Garden row inspector

    Drive between garden beds and snap photos of each plant. Check the photos for weeds and pests.

    Uses: Camera, Drives motors, Wi-Fi.

  • Classroom robotics lab

    Give each team a rover and a shared Viam account. Students race to finish a maze with Python code.

    Uses: Drives motors, Wi-Fi, Runs Linux. Add: cardboard maze walls.

Expert ideas
