Overview

This is a watch-shaped development board from Waveshare with a case and a strap. It has a 2.06 inch touchscreen, Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, two microphones, and a speaker. Makers use it for custom watch faces, smart home remotes, and voice projects. It suits hobbyists who already know a little Arduino or ESP32 coding.

Good fit or not?

Good for

  • Building your own smartwatch apps and watch faces
  • Touchscreen menus and remotes for smart home gear
  • Small voice and sound projects with built-in microphones and a speaker
  • Motion projects with the built-in six-axis sensor
  • Learning screen design with the LVGL graphics library

Not great for

  • Wiring up extra sensors. The spare pins sit on tiny pads with no headers.
  • People who want a finished smartwatch. The factory demo is basic.
  • Buyers who skip the battery version. The EN version ships with no battery.
  • Anyone hoping to avoid code. Every project needs some programming.

Specs in plain words

  • The dual-core 240 MHz chip runs a smooth touchscreen interface and Wi-Fi together.
  • 8 MB of extra memory holds full screen images and sound clips.
  • 32 MB of storage fits many pictures, fonts, and apps.
  • The 410 by 502 pixel AMOLED screen is sharp and bright with deep blacks.
  • The six-axis motion sensor feels tilts, shakes, and steps.
  • The watch body is about 5 by 4 centimeters and a bit over 1 centimeter thick.

Specs come from the Waveshare docs, the GitHub README and CNX Software. The size is the watch body from CNX Software. I2C, UART and USB lines are only on reserved pads. As a result, there are no user headers. The speaker entry covers the NS4150B amplifier output that CNX describes as a speaker. Battery capacity is not published.

Things you could build

15 ideas sized to what this board can do. Starter ideas suit first-time builders.

Starter ideas

  • Custom watch face

    Draw your own clock design on the bright round-cornered screen. Show the time, date, and battery level your way.

    Uses: Screen, Battery ready.

  • Music spectrum display

    Play music near the watch and watch colored bars dance to the beat. Waveshare ships an example to start from.

    Uses: Hears sound, Screen.

  • Wrist timer for workouts

    Set rounds of exercise and rest with a tap. The speaker beeps at each change.

    Uses: Screen, Makes sound, Battery ready.

Builder ideas

  • Step and motion counter

    The motion sensor counts steps and arm swings. The screen shows your daily total.

    Uses: Sensors built in, Screen, Battery ready.

  • Weather on your wrist

    The watch pulls a forecast over Wi-Fi. It shows rain or sun with a simple icon.

    Uses: Wi-Fi, Screen.

  • Smart home remote

    Tap buttons on the touchscreen to switch lights and plugs at home. The watch sends each command over Wi-Fi.

    Uses: Wi-Fi, Screen.

  • Pocket sound meter

    The microphones measure how loud a room is. The screen turns red in a noisy classroom or workshop.

    Uses: Hears sound, Screen.

  • Tiny wrist game

    Tilt your wrist to steer a ball through a maze on the screen. The motion sensor does the steering.

    Uses: Sensors built in, Screen.

  • Garden sensor viewer

    Outdoor sensors post soil and air readings to your network. Glance at your wrist to see if the plants need water.

    Uses: Wi-Fi, Screen. Add: soil moisture sensor, second Wi-Fi board.

  • Photo and video player

    Load pictures or short clips onto a microSD card. The watch plays them on its sharp screen.

    Uses: Screen. Add: microSD card.

  • Bike ride logger

    The watch records motion and time during a ride. It saves the log to a microSD card for graphing later.

    Uses: Sensors built in, Battery ready. Add: microSD card.

Expert ideas

  • Phone notification buddy

    Pair the watch with your phone over Bluetooth. Messages and calls pop up on your wrist.

    Uses: Bluetooth, Screen, Battery ready.

  • Voice command watch

    Speak a short command into the two microphones. The watch answers through its small speaker.

    Uses: Hears sound, Makes sound, Wi-Fi.

  • Fall alert for a loved one

    The motion sensor notices a sudden drop. The watch beeps and sends a message over Wi-Fi.

    Uses: Sensors built in, Wi-Fi, Makes sound.

  • Talking reminder watch

    The watch speaks reminders for medicine or homework at set times. The clock chip keeps time on battery.

    Uses: Makes sound, Battery ready, Screen.
